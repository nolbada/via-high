문제 본문 다시 보기
Unfortunately, sometimes the idea of a goal is ①connected from the daily reality of what it will take to achieve it. We fall in love with the idea of a goal, pushing out of mind how ② different our day-to-day will need to be for us to make it happen. In fact, research even suggests that positive fantasies about our future can lead us to put ③ less effort into our goals because our fantasies provide us with a ④ right and premature sense of accomplishment. We romanticize a goal — falling in love with the idea of a change. In the process, we ⑤ investigate the intricacies and challenges of integrating a complex new behavior into our life. As one study nicely summed it up, fantasizing about a positive future “⑥ forbids a person to mentally enjoy the desired future in the here and now” — and because we don’t reflect on why our fantasy is not yet our future, “a necessity to act is not ⑦ inhibited.”
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

① 2 → 2개

② 3 → 3개

③ 4 → 4개

④ 5 → 5개 (정답)

⑤ 6 → 6개

답의 근거 (본문)

Unfortunately, sometimes the idea of a goal is disconnected from the daily reality of what it will take to achieve it. (안타깝게도, 때때로 목표라는 생각은 그 목표를 이루는 데 필요한 일상의 현실과 동떨어져 있다.)

In fact, research even suggests that positive fantasies about our future can lead us to put less effort into our goals because our fantasies provide us with a false and premature sense of accomplishment. (실제로 연구는 미래에 대한 긍정적인 환상이 우리가 목표에 노력을 덜 쏟게 만들 수 있다고까지 말한다. 환상이 우리에게 가짜의, 너무 이른 성취감을 주기 때문이다.)

In the process, we gloss over the intricacies and challenges of integrating a complex new behavior into our life. (그 과정에서 우리는 복잡한 새 행동을 삶에 녹여 넣는 일의 복잡함과 어려움을 대충 얼버무리고 넘어간다.)

As one study nicely summed it up, fantasizing about a positive future “seduces a person to mentally enjoy the desired future in the here and now” — and because we don’t reflect on why our fantasy is not yet our future, “a necessity to act is not induced.” (한 연구가 잘 요약했듯이, 긍정적인 미래를 공상하는 것은 '사람을 꾀어 바라는 미래를 지금 여기에서 머릿속으로 즐기게' 만든다. 그리고 우리는 왜 환상이 아직 미래가 되지 않았는지 돌아보지 않기 때문에 '행동할 필요성이 생겨나지 않는다.')

쉬운 풀이

틀린 것은 5개예요.

① connected → disconnected (목표가 현실과 '동떨어져' 있다는 흐름, from과도 짝)

④ right → false (환상이 주는 건 '가짜' 성취감)

⑤ investigate → gloss over (어려움을 '조사하는' 게 아니라 '대충 넘긴다')

⑥ forbids → seduces (공상은 미래를 즐기도록 '꾀는' 것, 막는 게 아님)

⑦ inhibited → induced (행동할 필요가 '생겨나지' 않는다는 뜻이어야 함)

② different(다른), ③ less(더 적은)는 맞아요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 2개보다 많이 틀렸어요.

② 3개보다 많이 틀렸어요.

③ 4개보다 많이 틀렸어요.

⑤ ② different(다른), ③ less(더 적은)는 맞으니 6개가 아니에요.

시험 포인트

반의어 바꿔치기(connected/disconnected, false/right, seduces/forbids, induced/inhibited)를 찾는 문제예요. 특히 not inhibited(억제되지 않는다)는 정반대 뜻이 된다는 점에 주의해요.

다음 글의 밑줄 친 [a]~[e] 중 문맥상 낱말의 쓰임이 가장 어색한 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Since hydra and the immortal jellyfish don’t [a]exhibit obvious signs of aging, they are often called biologically immortal. This doesn’t mean they don’t die. Nor does it even mean that they cannot die of biological causes. But, [b]unlike most every animal, their likelihood of dying does not increase with age. Species such as hydra and the immortal jellyfish excite gerontologists because they may provide clues about how to [c]defeat the aging process. But their property of being able to regenerate entire body parts, or even a whole organism, makes them more similar to trees than to us. Although we may learn some fascinating things about their lack of apparent aging, it is not at all [d]uncertain how relevant those findings will be to human aging. Sometimes biology is universal, especially if it relates to fundamental mechanisms. But in other cases, even discoveries in rats or mice, which are mammals and biologically much [e]closer to us, are difficult to translate into humans. It may be a very long time before any findings gained from hydra or jellyfish are useful to us.
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

① [a] exhibit → 보이다, 드러내다. 노화 징후를 보이지 않는다는 뜻으로 맞아요.

② [b] unlike → ~와 달리. 거의 모든 동물과 달리라는 뜻으로 맞아요.

③ [c] defeat → 물리치다. 노화 과정을 물리친다는 뜻으로 맞아요.

④ [d] uncertain → 불확실한. 'not at all uncertain'(전혀 불확실하지 않은)이면 '전혀 불확실하지 않다(아주 확실하다)'가 되어 흐름과 반대예요.
