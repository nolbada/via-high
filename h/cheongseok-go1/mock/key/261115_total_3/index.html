다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Among unsatisfied professionals, there’s a repeating theme — the belief that someday (a mysterious day in the future) things will miraculously change and they’ll stumble into the perfect job or career. At the same time, they (typically) do little to meaningfully alter their current situation. They don’t like their job, so what do they do? They head to job boards and search for roles with a similar job title, hoping that the universe will sprinkle a bit of magic on their career if they just hit the refresh button in a different department or company. Efforts like this make professionals feel like they are doing something, when really they’re just setting themselves up to take a new role and quickly become just as disenchanted as they were before. It’s like rearranging deck chairs on the Titanic. You’ve got to completely switch up your approach. If you __________, you’ll win the same prizes.
정답: ③

보기와 우리말 뜻

① refuse to accept external help from career mentors → 진로 멘토의 외부 도움을 거절하다

② blindly follow commercial employment trends → 상업적인 고용 유행을 맹목적으로 따르다

③ repeatedly execute the exact same flawed pattern of action → 똑같은 잘못된 행동 패턴을 반복해서 실행하다

④ abandon your long-term occupational dreams too early → 장기적인 직업 꿈을 너무 일찍 포기하다

⑤ drastically remodel your work ethic and daily routines → 직업 윤리와 일과를 크게 바꾸다

답의 근거 (본문)

You’ve got to completely switch up your approach. (접근 방식을 완전히 바꿔야 해요.)

If you keep playing the same game, you’ll win the same prizes. (같은 게임을 계속하면 같은 상품을 받게 돼요.)

쉬운 풀이

빈칸 문장은 "~하면, 같은 상품을 받게 된다"예요.

원래 문장은 keep playing the same game(같은 게임을 계속하다)이에요.

같은 결과가 나오는 이유는 같은 방식을 반복하기 때문이니 ③이 딱 맞아요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 멘토의 도움 얘기는 글에 없어요.

② 유행을 따른다는 얘기는 없어요.

④ 꿈을 포기하는 게 문제가 아니라 같은 방식을 반복하는 게 문제예요.

⑤ 크게 바꾸면 오히려 다른 결과가 나와요. 글의 조언과 같은 쪽이라 빈칸과 반대예요.

시험 포인트

same game(같은 게임) → same prizes(같은 결과)의 반복 구조를 패러프레이즈한 보기를 찾는 문제예요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
In recent years, the number of illegal actions in particular at the corporate level has grown, or at least the number of cases that came to light, increased. Such misconduct has often only been exposed because brave informants have risked a great deal in order to put an end to it. Hence, it is no surprise that whistleblower protection has come into focus. ①If violations of law and order go undetected and unpunished for years because employees of a company fear to report illegal activities for fear of reprisals or consequences, it can have several negative effects. ②It can harm the company itself, but also third-parties, the economy in general, the general public, and even the rule of law could be affected. ③The disclosure of internal misconduct for the public good is often abused as a means of leaking corporate trade secrets. ④Protecting those who would assist in reducing unlawful or even criminal behaviour has two main functions: Protecting the individual’s actions as well as protecting the public good. ⑤A well-balanced whistleblower protection may have positive effects on both of these.

보기와 우리말 뜻
