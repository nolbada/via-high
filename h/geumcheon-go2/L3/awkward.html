문맥 1번 글어색한 곳 3개

We make many collective1 choices in our lives, from who to have lunch with to what career to pursue. However, there are many situations where we must make decisions as a group. Group decision-making is not as simple as our personal choices, as it dismisses2 numerous voices with differing opinions. Voting is often used to make group decisions fairer and more efficient, and various voting methods have been developed over time. The following story illustrates how these different voting methods work.

The members of the Greenville High School student council needed to decide how to raise money for children in need. Their three options were an art show, a bake sale, and a charity concert, but there were consistent3 opinions. So they decided to put it to a vote. When they asked for advice from Ms. Akers, the council’s advisor, she encouraged them not to use the familiar voting method, but to try something different instead. After doing some research, the council members decided to try three different voting systems: the first-past-the-post (FPTP) method, the alternative vote (AV) method, and the Condorcet method. The entire council was curious to find out if the different methods would yield different results.

글의 흐름 개인의 선택과 집단의 결정을 견주며 시작해서, 학생 자치회가 투표 방법을 고르는 이야기로 넘어가요. However(하지만)와 but(하지만)이 방향을 바꾸는 자리이니, 앞뒤가 서로 반대되는지 보세요.
그대로 두는 부분 fairer (더 공정한)는 투표가 집단 결정을 '더 공정하고 더 효율적으로' 만든다는 말이라 맞아요. familiar (익숙한)는 Akers 선생님이 '익숙한 투표 방법 말고 다른 것을 해 보라'고 권했다는 뜻이라 맞고, curious (궁금한)는 '다른 방법이 다른 결과를 낼지 궁금해했다'는 뜻이라 그대로 둬요.

문맥 2번 글The First-Past-The-Post Method (단순 다수결 방식) · 어색한 곳 3개

The FPTP method is the simplest of the three methods. Voters are given several options, and whichever gets the most first-preference votes wins. Neither the second nor third preference matters at all, so they are considered1. This method is commonly used in political elections around the world.

The student council quickly determined that the winner would be option C, the charity concert. This was because it received 8 first-preference votes, while option A received 5 and option B received 7.

The FPTP method is uncomplicated and easy to understand, but it has a significant strength2. It is impossible3 to win a vote without the support of the majority. That was the case here. According to the results of the FPTP method, the council’s choice was to hold a charity concert. However, only 8 out of 20 council members chose the charity concert as their first preference, which equaled 40% of the total votes.

글의 흐름 세 가지 투표 방식 중 첫 번째인 FPTP를 소개하는 글이에요. 장점을 말한 뒤 but(하지만)으로 약점을 말하고, 8표만으로 이긴 사례가 나와요. 표가 무시되는지 고려되는지, 강점인지 약점인지, 가능한지 불가능한지를 보세요.
그대로 두는 부분 simplest (가장 간단한)는 '세 방식 중 가장 간단하다'는 설명이라 맞고, commonly (흔히)는 '전 세계 정치 선거에서 흔히 쓰인다'는 내용이라 맞아요. only (오직)는 '20명 중 8명만'이라는 뜻이라 그대로 둬요.

문맥 3번 글The Alternative Vote Method (대안 투표 방식) · 어색한 곳 3개

Next, the council members examined the votes using the AV method, and they soon realized that there would be a different result. The AV method can potentially exclude1 more than one round of counting. For an option to win in the first round, it needs to get more than 50% of the first-preference votes. As the charity concert only received 40% of the total votes, a second round of counting was required.

Option A, the art show, received the fewest first-preference votes, with only 5 council members voting for it. Therefore, option A was selected2, and the 5 council members’ votes were redistributed based on their second preferences. As a result, 4 votes for option B (ABC) and 1 for option C (ACB) were added to the total. This meant that option B had 11 total votes, while option C had only 9. As a result, the bake sale would be the winner.

Fortunately3, the AV method is not perfect either. It sometimes allows second-preference votes to have a greater influence than first-preference votes. In this case, the bake sale won, but this was mainly due to the second-preference votes it received.

글의 흐름 두 번째 투표 방식인 AV를 설명하는 글이에요. 첫 집계에서 과반이 안 나오면 꼴찌를 빼고 표를 다시 나눠요. 집계가 늘어나는지 줄어드는지, 꼴찌 선택지가 뽑히는지 빠지는지, 좋은 소식인지 나쁜 소식인지를 보세요.
그대로 두는 부분 different (다른)는 '다른 결과가 나올 것'이라는 뜻이라 맞고, required (필요한)는 '2차 집계가 필요했다'는 뜻이라 맞아요. fewest (가장 적은)는 'A가 가장 적은 1지망 표를 받았다'는 뜻이라 그대로 둬요.
(1)어색한 말exclude (제외하다) → include (포함하다)
고친 문장The AV method can potentially include more than one round of counting.
AV 방식은 잠재적으로 한 차례 이상의 집계를 포함할 수 있다.
The AV method can potentially include more than one round of counting(AV 방식은 잠재적으로 한 차례 이상의 집계를 포함할 수 있다)이 원문이에요. 바로 뒤에서 첫 집계에서 이기려면 50%를 넘어야 하는데 40%라서 a second round of counting(2차 집계)이 필요했다고 해요. 집계가 여러 번 있다는 내용이니 include가 맞아요. exclude (제외하다)면 2차 집계가 나오는 뒤 내용과 부딪쳐요.

문맥 4번 글The Condorcet Method (콩도르세 방식) · 어색한 곳 3개

Finally, the student council counted the votes using the Condorcet method. This method involves comparing the voters’ preferences between each possible pair of options. In this case, there were three pairs to consider: A vs. B, B vs. C, and A vs. C.

Through this method, the student council discovered that its members preferred option A to option B by a count of 11 to 9. In other words, 11 people preferred A to B, voting either ABC, ACB, or CAB. Meanwhile, 9 people preferred B to A by voting BAC, BCA, or CBA. Interestingly, the voters preferred option B to option C by the same count of 11 to 9. They also preferred option A to option C by 11 to 9. As option A, the art show, was preferred to both of the other options, it would be considered the loser1.

The Condorcet method’s weakness is that, along with being simple2, it sometimes fails to select a winner. This is especially true in elections that lack3 numerous options. It is possible that no option will be clearly preferred over the others.

글의 흐름 세 번째 방식인 콩도르세를 설명하는 글이에요. 선택지를 둘씩 짝지어 비교해요. 모든 비교에서 이긴 쪽을 뭐라고 부르는지, 약점을 말하는 문장에 장점이 끼어 있지 않은지, 선택지가 많은지 적은지를 보세요.
그대로 두는 부분 involves (수반하다)는 '가능한 모든 쌍을 비교하는 것을 수반한다'는 설명이라 맞아요. Interestingly (흥미롭게도)는 '세 비교가 모두 똑같이 11대 9'라는 신기한 결과를 소개하는 말이라 맞고, weakness (약점)는 '때때로 승자를 못 가린다'는 내용과 이어져 그대로 둬요.
(1)어색한 말loser (패자) → winner (승자)
고친 문장As option A, the art show, was preferred to both of the other options, it would be considered the winner.
미술 전시회인 선택지 A가 나머지 두 선택지보다 더 선호되었으므로, 이 선택지가 승자로 간주될 것이었다.
it would be considered the winner(그 선택지는 승자로 간주될 것이다)가 원문이에요. A는 B보다도 11대 9로, C보다도 11대 9로 선호됐어요. 앞에 As option A ... was preferred to both of the other options(A가 나머지 두 선택지보다 더 선호되었으므로)라는 이유가 있으니 결과는 승자예요. loser (패자)면 모든 비교에서 이긴 선택지를 패자라고 하는 셈이라 말이 안 돼요.
↑ 빠른정답
(2)어색한 말simple (간단한) → complex (복잡한)

어법 5번 글The Condorcet Method (콩도르세 방식) · 어색한 곳 3개

Finally, the student council counted the votes using the Condorcet method. This method involves comparing the voters’ preferences between each possible pair of options. In this case, there were three pairs to consider: A vs. B, B vs. C, and A vs. C.

Through this method, the student council discovered that its members preferred option A to option B by a count of 11 to 9. In other words, 11 people preferred A to B, voting either ABC, ACB, or CAB. Meanwhile, 9 people preferred B to A by voting BAC, BCA, or CBA. Interestingly, the voters preferred option B to option C by the same count of 11 to 9. They also preferred option A to option C by 11 to 9. As option A, the art show, was preferred to both of the other options, it would be considered the winner.

The Condorcet method’s weakness is that, along with being complex, it sometimes fails selecting a winner1. This is especially true in elections that contains numerous options2. It is possible that no option will clearly prefer over the others3.
