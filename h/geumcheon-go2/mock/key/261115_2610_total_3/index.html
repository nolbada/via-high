For example, courts are traditional social institutions and important for the normal and harmonious functioning of collective life. However, when people turn to social networks to put pressure on judicial decisions in certain mediated and politicized processes, this practice is popularized and becomes a habitual form of culture, which is harmful. Justice must not be done in the public square, which today is converted into a virtual dimension, the social networks. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood seen as a period of stability and old age as a phase of decline. 보기와 우리말 뜻

① Human development follows a predetermined pattern that remains largely unchanged after the completion of childhood. (인간 발달은 어린 시절 이후 거의 바뀌지 않는 정해진 패턴을 따른다.) → 옛 관점이라 틀려요.

⑤ The possibility of personal transformation is restricted to the early years, while later periods are characterized by irreversible decline. (개인 변화 가능성은 어린 시절에만 있고, 이후는 되돌릴 수 없는 쇠퇴가 특징이다.) → 옛 관점이라 틀려요.

답의 근거 (본문)

그리고 이 변화는 전 생애에 걸쳐 계속돼요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly understood as an ongoing, flexible, and evolving process rather than a ❶fixed sequence of stages. At the core of this perspective lies the concept of plasticity. Plasticity refers to the human capacity to change, adapt, and reorganize cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to life experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. It highlights that individuals are not merely ❷dynamic recipients of development but active agents capable of growth throughout their lives. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood seen as a period of ❸stability and old age as a phase of decline. The concept of plasticity ❹challenges this rigid outlook by emphasizing that development continues across the entire lifespan. Although the extent of change may vary at different stages, every phase of life ❺allows room for adaptation and transformation.
정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

① ❶ fixed (고정된) → ‘고정된 단계의 연속이 아니라’로 맞아요.

② ❷ dynamic (역동적인) → 뒤의 active agents(능동적인 주체)와 대조되려면 passive(수동적인)여야 해요.

③ ❸ stability (안정) → 옛 관점에서 성인기는 안정기라 맞아요.

④ ❹ challenges (이의를 제기하다) → 가소성이 옛 관점에 반대하니 맞아요.

⑤ ❺ allows (허용하다) → 모든 단계가 변화의 여지를 준다는 뜻이라 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

It highlights that individuals are not merely passive recipients of development but active agents capable of growth throughout their lives. (그것은 개인이 단지 발달의 수동적인 수용자가 아니라, 평생 성장할 수 있는 능동적인 주체라는 점을 강조해요.)

쉬운 풀이

그래서 정답은 ②예요.

① rather than a fixed sequence(고정된 연속이라기보다)로 흐름에 맞아요.

④ plasticity(가소성)가 rigid outlook(경직된 관점)에 반대하니 challenges(이의를 제기하다)가 맞아요.
