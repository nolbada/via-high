A person who lives eighty years lives some twenty-nine thousand days, and the overwhelming majority of the behaviour packed into them runs on settings laid down, most of them, decades earlier and rarely inspected since. (A) Therefore the default is not a flaw in the human design. It is arguably the central feature, the thing that makes a complex life affordable at all. (B) The deliberate mind is far too slow and too costly to run a whole life in real time, and any creature that tried would be outcompeted by a creature that didn’t. The automation is precisely what frees the captain to come up on deck for the few problems that actually require him. (C) This need not be a hopeless thought. 정답: ⑤

보기와 우리말 뜻

① (A) - (C) - (B) → (A)의 Therefore(그러므로) 결론이 맨 앞에 올 수 없어요.

② (B) - (A) - (C) → (C)의 This need not be a hopeless thought(절망적일 필요 없다)가 맨 끝에 오면 This(이것)가 가리킬 것이 멀어져요.

③ (B) - (C) - (A) → 이유(B)보다 관점 전환(C)이 먼저 나와야 해요.

④ (C) - (A) - (B) → 결론(A) 뒤에 이유(B)가 오면 Therefore(그러므로)가 어색해요.

⑤ (C) - (B) - (A) → 절망할 필요 없다, 침대에서 못 나온다(C) → 의식은 너무 느리고 자동화가 선장을 해방(B) → 그러므로 결함이 아니다(A). 자연스러워요.

The deliberate mind is far too slow and too costly to run a whole life in real time, and any creature that tried would be outcompeted by a creature that didn’t. (의식적인 생각은 삶 전체를 실시간으로 굴리기엔 너무 느리고 비용이 커요. 그렇게 하려는 생물은 그러지 않는 생물에게 경쟁에서 질 거예요.)

(C)의 This(이것)가 이 사실을 받아 절망할 필요 없다고 하고, 다 의식하면 침대에서 못 나온다고 해요.

(B)는 그 이유로 의식은 너무 느리다며 자동화의 장점을 말해요.

(A)는 Therefore(그러므로)로 기본 설정은 결함이 아니라고 결론을 내요.

그래서 정답은 ⑤예요.

② This(이것)가 주어진 글을 바로 받아야 해요.

③ (C)가 주어진 글 바로 뒤에 와야 해요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly understood as an ongoing, flexible, and evolving process rather than a fixed sequence of stages. At the core of this perspective lies the concept of plasticity. Plasticity refers to the human capacity to change, adapt, and reorganize cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to life experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. It highlights that individuals are not merely passive recipients of development but active agents capable of growth throughout their lives. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood seen as a period of stability and old age as a phase of decline. The concept of plasticity challenges this rigid outlook by emphasizing that development continues across the entire lifespan. Although the extent of change may vary at different stages, every phase of life allows room for adaptation and transformation. Human beings possess an (A)_________ capacity for personal growth and transformation, allowing them to (B)_________ their characteristics and overcome limitations through diverse circumstances at every age.
정답: ③

보기와 우리말 뜻

문제 본문 다시 보기
Dolphins’ brains have evolved specialized areas dedicated to echolocation, allowing them to process acoustic information more quickly and with less energy. One key adaptation is the structure of the dolphin’s auditory pathway. The signals from the ears are rapidly transmitted to the auditory cortex, the brain region responsible for processing sound. However, unlike many other mammals, dolphins have a highly specialized auditory cortex with distinct areas dedicated to processing different aspects of echolocation signals. Some areas may be responsible for analyzing the timing of echoes, while others focus on the frequency content or the intensity of the sound. This specialization allows the dolphin’s brain to process echo information in parallel, rather than sequentially. Imagine a factory assembly line, where different workers are responsible for different tasks. By dividing the work among specialized areas, the brain can process information much faster and more efficiently. This parallel processing reduces the cognitive load associated with echolocation, allowing dolphins to react quickly to changes in their environment. Thanks to the (A) __________ organization of its auditory cortex, a dolphin’s brain handles several features of an echo (B) ____________, which lightens its mental workload.
정답: ⑤

보기와 우리말 뜻

① divided - successively (나뉜 - 연속적으로) → (B)가 반대예요.

② uniform - concurrently (균일한 - 동시에) → (A)가 반대예요.

③ segmented - repeatedly (구획된 - 반복적으로) → (B)가 틀려요.

④ simplified - selectively (단순화된 - 선택적으로) → 둘 다 글과 달라요.

⑤ partitioned - at once (분할된 - 한꺼번에) → 둘 다 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

This specialization allows the dolphin’s brain to process echo information in parallel, rather than sequentially. (이런 특화 덕분에 돌고래의 뇌는 메아리 정보를 순서대로가 아니라 동시에(병렬로) 처리해요.)

쉬운 풀이

(A) 청각 피질에는 distinct areas(별도 구역)가 있어요.

그래서 구역이 나뉜 partitioned(분할된)가 맞아요.

(B) 뇌는 메아리의 여러 특징을 in parallel(병렬로), 즉 at once(한꺼번에) 처리해요.

그래서 정답은 ⑤예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① (A)는 맞지만 successively(연속적으로)는 sequentially(순차적으로)와 같아서 반대예요.

② uniform(균일한)은 구역이 나뉜 것과 반대예요.

③ (A)는 맞지만 repeatedly(반복적으로)는 병렬 처리와 상관없어요.

④ simplified(단순화된), selectively(선택적으로)는 글에 없어요.
