문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly understood as an ongoing, flexible, and evolving process rather than a fixed sequence of stages. At the core of this perspective lies the concept of plasticity. Plasticity refers to the human capacity to change, adapt, and reorganize cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to life experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. It highlights that individuals are not merely passive recipients of development but active agents capable of growth throughout their lives. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood seen as a period of stability and old age as a phase of decline. The concept of plasticity challenges this rigid outlook by emphasizing that development continues across the entire lifespan. Although the extent of change may vary at different stages, every phase of life allows room for adaptation and transformation.
정답: ⑤

보기와 우리말 뜻

① limitations of human adaptability to environmental changes (환경 변화에 대한 인간 적응성의 한계) → 글은 적응하는 능력을 말해요. 한계 얘기는 없어요.

② the critical importance of early childhood in human development (인간 발달에서 유아기의 결정적 중요성) → 아동기만 중요하다는 옛 관점을 오히려 비판해요.

③ a historical overview of traditional perspectives on developmental stages (발달 단계에 대한 전통적 관점의 역사적 개요) → 옛 관점은 비교하려고 잠깐 나올 뿐이에요.

④ various factors contributing to cognitive and emotional decline in old age (노년기 인지·정서 쇠퇴에 기여하는 여러 요인) → 쇠퇴의 원인은 나오지 않아요.

⑤ the pivotal role of plasticity in redefining human development as a lifelong process (인간 발달을 평생의 과정으로 다시 정의하는 데 가소성이 하는 핵심 역할) → 글 전체와 같아요.

다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly ① viewed as a continuous, flexible, and evolving process rather than a fixed sequence of stages. At the heart of this viewpoint ② lies the concept of plasticity. Plasticity refers to the human capacity to change, adapt, and reorganize cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to life experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. It highlights that individuals are not merely passive recipients of development but active agents ③ capable of growth throughout their lives. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood ④ seeing as a period of stability and old age as a phase of decline. The concept of plasticity challenges this rigid outlook by emphasizing that development continues across the entire lifespan. ⑤ Despite differences in the extent of change across life stages, every phase of life allows room for adaptation and transformation.
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

① viewed (view의 과거분사, '여겨지는') → 맞아요. 발달이 '여겨지는' 수동이라 is viewed가 돼요.

② lies (lie의 3인칭 단수형, '놓여 있다') → 맞아요. 주어 the concept가 단수이고, 도치된 문장이에요.

③ capable ('할 수 있는') → 맞아요. active agents를 뒤에서 꾸미는 형용사예요.

④ seeing ('보는') → 틀려요. adulthood는 '여겨지는' 대상이라 seen이 맞아요.

⑤ Despite ('~에도 불구하고') → 맞아요. 뒤에 명사구 differences가 와서 전치사가 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood (예전 관점에서는 발달이 주로 아동기에 일어난다고 믿었어요)

At the heart of this viewpoint (이 관점의 중심에)

active agents (능동적인 주체)

쉬운 풀이

④가 있는 부분은 with adulthood seeing as a period of stability(성인기를 안정기로 '보는' 채)예요. with + 명사 + 분사 구문에서 분사는 명사와의 관계로 정해요. 성인기는 안정기로 '여겨지는' 대상이에요. 그래서 수동의 과거분사 seen이 맞아요. 예를 들어 He sat with his arms crossed.(그는 팔짱을 낀 채 앉아 있었어요.)에서도 팔은 '꼬아지는' 쪽이라 crossed를 써요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① is viewed as ~는 '~로 여겨진다'는 수동이에요. He is viewed as a hero.(그는 영웅으로 여겨져요.)처럼 써요. 맞아요.

② 장소 표현이 앞으로 나가 도치된 문장이에요. 주어는 단수 the concept라 lies가 맞아요. On the table lies a book.(탁자 위에 책이 한 권 놓여 있어요.)과 같아요.

③ capable of growth는 '성장할 수 있는'이라는 뜻이에요. 형용사가 agents를 뒤에서 꾸며 맞아요.

⑤ Despite 뒤에는 명사(구)가 와요. Despite the rain, we played.(비가 왔는데도 우리는 놀았어요.)처럼요. 뒤에 절이 오면 Although를 써요.

시험 포인트

어법 문제는 with + 명사 + 분사 구문이 단골이에요. 명사가 동작을 하면 -ing, 동작을 받으면 p.p.로 골라요. 이 지문의 adulthood는 '여겨지는' 쪽이라 seen이에요.

정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly viewed as a continuous, flexible, and evolving process rather than a fixed sequence of stages. At the heart of this viewpoint lies the concept of plasticity. ① Plasticity is the ability of humans to modify and adjust their cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to their experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. ② It emphasizes that people actively shape their own development and can continue to grow throughout their lives rather than simply receiving developmental influences passively. ③ Although earlier views held that development occurred mainly during childhood, followed by stability in adulthood and decline in old age, the concept of plasticity emphasizes that development continues across the entire lifespan. ④ Lifelong growth is more likely to occur in environments that embrace diversity and change, highlighting the importance of encountering different perspectives throughout life. ⑤ Although the degree of change may vary across different stages, each period of life provides opportunities for adjustment and growth.
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

① ① Plasticity is the ability of humans to modify and adjust … (가소성은 사람이 경험에 맞춰 생각, 감정, 행동을 바꾸고 조절하는 능력이에요) → 앞 문장의 plasticity를 정의해서 자연스러워요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Social media has altered business, politics, and friendships, making going viral a household term. The global financial industry links every bank and countless households in every country, making us vulnerable to human error taking place on the other side of the world. As Columbia University strategy professor Rita McGrath explains, years ago most of our institutions were separate and thereby buffered from the effects of errors outside their walls. That is no longer the case. The digitization of massive amounts of information has continued to grow enormously as the cost of computing power has decreased. The development of smart systems that communicate autonomously gave rise to an infinite variety of potential breakdowns. This interdependence is ㉠______________________________. As Rita puts it, when “things that used to be kept separate bump up against each other, it becomes far more difficult to predict what’s going to happen next.” Information technology creates new vulnerabilities because interconnectedness instantly spreads the impact of small failures. *vulnerable: 취약한 **buffer: 보호하다
정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

① a collection of minor, unrelated errors (작고 서로 관련 없는 오류들의 모음) → 서로 얽힌 구조라 '관련 없는'은 반대예요.

② a breeding ground for complex failure (복잡한 실패의 온상) → 얽힘이 예측하기 어려운 실패를 키운다는 흐름과 같아요.

③ a source of failures that are easy to foresee (예측하기 쉬운 실패의 원천) → 뒤에서 예측이 far more difficult(훨씬 더 어려운)라고 해요.

④ a dependable engine of efficiency and innovation (효율과 혁신을 이끄는 믿을 만한 원동력) → 뒤는 새 취약성 얘기라 긍정적인 말과 맞지 않아요.

⑤ a temporary problem that new technology will fix (새 기술이 해결해 줄 일시적인 문제) → 기술이 오히려 취약성을 만든다고 해요.

답의 근거 (본문)

years ago most of our institutions were separate and thereby buffered from the effects of errors outside their walls (몇 년 전만 해도 대부분의 기관은 분리되어 있어서 울타리 밖 오류의 영향에서 보호받았어요)

쉬운 풀이

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 상호 의존이면 오류들이 서로 이어져요. unrelated(관련 없는)는 반대예요.

③ 뒤에서 예측하기가 far more difficult(훨씬 더 어려운)라고 해요. easy to foresee(예측하기 쉬운)는 반대예요.

④ 효율과 혁신은 본문에 없어요. 글 분위기는 위험 쪽이에요.

⑤ temporary(일시적인)가 틀려요. 글은 새로운 취약성이 생겼다고 말해요.

(A) The digitization of massive amounts of information has continued to grow enormously as the cost of computing power has decreased. 보기와 우리말 뜻

① (A) - (C) - (B) → 주어진 글 뒤에 (A)의 digitization이 갑자기 나와요. 또 (B)의 That is no longer the case가 마지막에 와서 어색해요.

② (B) - (A) - (C) → 주어진 글 → 옛날엔 분리됨(B) → 지금은 디지털화로 연결(A) → 이 상호 의존의 결과(C) 순서예요.

④ (C) - (A) - (B) → 주어진 글 뒤에 This interdependence가 바로 오면 가리키는 대상이 없어요.

⑤ (C) - (B) - (A) → (C)가 맨 앞이면 지시어가 어색하고, (C)의 결론이 중간에 와서 흐름이 깨져요.

답의 근거 (본문)

That is no longer the case. (컬럼비아 대학 전략 교수 리타 맥그래스가 설명하듯, 몇 년 전에는 대부분의 기관이 분리되어 있어서 울타리 밖 오류의 영향에서 보호받았어요. 지금은 더 이상 그렇지 않아요.)

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① (A)는 처음 나오는 digitization이라 연결어가 없어요. (B)가 맨 끝이면 That is no longer the case(지금은 더 이상 그렇지 않다)가 대비할 내용도 없어요.

③ (C)의 This interdependence가 가리킬 연결 설명이 (A)에 있어요. (B) 뒤에 바로 (C)가 오면 근거가 부족해요.

④ 주어진 글 바로 뒤에 This interdependence(이 상호 의존)가 오면 가리키는 말이 없어요. 또 Rita McGrath는 (B)에서 먼저 소개돼야 해요.

⑤ (C)가 맨 앞이면 This interdependence의 대상이 없어요. 또 (C)의 As Rita puts it(리타가 말하듯)은 (B)에서 소개된 뒤에 나와야 해요.

Social media has altered business, politics, and friendships, making going viral a ① household term. The global financial industry links every bank and ② countless households in every country, making us vulnerable to human error taking place on the other side of the world. As Columbia University strategy professor Rita McGrath explains, years ago most of our institutions were separate and thereby buffered from the effects of errors outside their walls. 정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

① household: familiar and commonly known to most people (대부분의 사람에게 익숙하고 널리 알려진) → household term(흔한 말)에 맞아요.

② countless: too many to be counted; innumerable (너무 많아서 셀 수 없는) → 맞아요.

③ autonomously: operating independently without external control (외부 통제 없이 독립적으로 작동하는) → 맞아요.

④ breeding ground: a carefully controlled environment designed to eliminate risks and prevent failure (위험을 없애고 실패를 막도록 세심히 통제된 환경) → 틀려요. 실제 뜻은 나쁜 일이 쉽게 생기고 퍼지는 곳이에요.

⑤ vulnerabilities: the quality of being open to attack or harm (공격이나 피해에 노출되는 성질) → 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

because interconnectedness instantly spreads the impact of small failures (연결성이 작은 실패의 영향을 순식간에 퍼뜨리기 때문이에요)

쉬운 풀이

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① household term은 집집마다 아는 흔한 말이에요. 풀이가 맞아요.

② countless는 '셀 수 없이 많은'이에요. 풀이가 맞아요.

③ autonomously는 '스스로, 자율적으로'예요. 풀이가 맞아요.

⑤ vulnerability는 '취약성'이에요. 본문의 vulnerabilities는 복수형이에요. 풀이가 맞아요.

The discovery of penicillin in 1928 by Alexander Fleming, for which he later received the Nobel Prize, was one of the biggest breakthroughs in the battle against infectious diseases. Edward Jenner discovered around 1796 that humans were protected against the deadly smallpox virus after exposure to the cowpox virus, which is harmless to humans. Vaccines developed in laboratories have contributed enormously to the reduction of the burden of bacterial and viral diseases and have even led to the elimination of poxvirus. For other infectious diseases (for example cholera and malaria), control and hygiene management measures have reduced their burden, while for others treatment therapies turned viral diseases that were deadly into chronic manageable diseases.
