문제 본문 다시 보기
To see how borrowing and lending changes a person’s worldview, consider what the rate of interest does to people’s perception of time. Imagine you are lending to somebody at an interest rate of 10 percent per year for five years. This rate tells us that the money lent has a cost that reflects the risk of you not being repaid and the opportunity cost of not spending that money yourself. The longer the term of the loan, the higher the chance you won’t be paid back because it is further into the future ― which by definition is unknowable ― and the longer you will have to wait before spending the money yourself. In order to make this worthwhile for the lender, money has to have a price ― a cost to the borrower and income to the lender. This price factors in the value of time. To put it another way, a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.
정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

① Shortening loan maturities guarantees substantial economic growth for lenders. (대출 만기를 줄이면 빌려주는 사람에게 큰 경제 성장이 보장된다.) → 경제 성장 보장 이야기는 없어요.

② Interest rates embody the compensation required for enduring the risks and deferred usage of time. (이자율은 위험과 시간상 미뤄진 사용을 견디는 데 필요한 보상을 담고 있다.) → 글의 핵심과 같아요.

③ People's perception of time remains largely detached from financial transactions. (사람들의 시간 인식은 금융 거래와 대체로 동떨어져 있다.) → 이자율이 시간 인식에 영향을 준다고 해서 반대예요.

④ Borrowers and lenders share identical financial motivations regarding investment yields. (빌리는 사람과 빌려주는 사람은 투자 수익에 대해 똑같은 동기를 가진다.) → 한쪽엔 비용, 한쪽엔 수입이라 달라요.

⑤ Unpredictable future markets render interest-based lending economically irrational. (예측할 수 없는 미래 시장 때문에 이자 대출은 경제적으로 비합리적이다.) → 오히려 그래서 이자가 필요하다고 해요.

밑줄 친 “would not be nearly as inclusive as we would want it to be today”가 다음 글에서 의미하는 바로 가장 적절한 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Consistent though he may be, some of Aristotle’s ideas meet us today as strange. He had no difficulty justifying slavery, for example, and in his era, philosophers enjoyed positions of high privilege and influence. Because of their practice of contemplation for the highest human good, philosophers were considered worthy to have all the best things in life. Somehow that assertion seems oddly self-serving. It serves us to remember that every great thinker is of her own time and speaks to a particular audience, and certainly Aristotle is no exception. Human flourishing as he understood it probably would not be nearly as inclusive as we would want it to be today. How he understood right and good is best taken to be an indicator of how the privileged elite of ancient Greek society tended to understand right and good. As a result, for all it does well, it is also deeply flawed.
정답: ③

보기와 우리말 뜻

① Aristotle’s conception of human flourishing anticipated modern ideals of equal rights and social inclusion. (그의 인간 번영 개념은 평등권과 사회적 포용이라는 현대 이상을 앞서 내다봤다.) → 반대예요.

② The pursuit of human flourishing was considered attainable only through intellectual discipline and moral reflection. (인간 번영은 지적 훈련과 도덕적 성찰로만 이룰 수 있다고 여겨졌다.) → 밑줄의 뜻이 아니에요.

③ Aristotle’s view of a good life reflected social boundaries that conflict with contemporary ideals of inclusiveness. (그의 좋은 삶에 대한 관점은 현대의 포용 이상과 충돌하는 사회적 경계를 반영했다.) → 밑줄의 뜻과 같아요.

④ The meaning of human flourishing has remained largely unchanged despite shifts in social values and norms. (사회적 가치가 변해도 인간 번영의 의미는 거의 그대로였다.) → 오늘날과 다르다고 했어요.

⑤ Aristotle’s ethical framework prioritized individual fulfillment over the collective interests of the broader community. (그의 윤리 체계는 공동체 이익보다 개인의 성취를 앞세웠다.) → 개인 대 공동체 이야기는 없어요.

답의 근거 (본문)

How he understood right and good is best taken to be an indicator of how the privileged elite of ancient Greek society tended to understand right and good. (그가 옳음과 선을 이해한 방식은, 고대 그리스 사회의 특권층 엘리트가 옳음과 선을 이해하던 방식을 보여 주는 지표로 보는 것이 가장 좋아요.)

바로 뒤에서 그의 생각은 고대 그리스 privileged elite(특권층)의 생각을 보여 준다고 해요.

즉 모든 사람이 아니라 일부만 좋은 삶을 누리는 경계가 있었다는 거예요.

③의 social boundaries(사회적 경계)와 conflict with contemporary ideals of inclusiveness(현대의 포용 이상과 충돌)가 이것을 담아요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
A study by Eva McMahan, professor of speech communication, confirmed the dominance of nonverbal signals. The phenomenon, however, was especially pronounced when adults found themselves in situations with conflicting messages (i.e., situations in which the contents of the verbal and nonverbal messages differ). Attempts to manipulate another person verbally are therefore likely to fail if the accompanying nonverbal communication does not match the spoken message. This is because people trust nonverbal messages more than verbal ones. When somebody tells you that they enjoy spending time with you, but you notice how they are looking away and are forcing a smile, you are probably less inclined to believe their words. The same is probably true when you are back at the office after a sickness and your colleague tells you with a frowning expression that they were happy to take over your workload. You might not believe that they enjoyed replacing you while you were gone.
정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

① Spoken statements consistently hold primary authority over physical indicators in human communication. (말이 몸의 신호보다 항상 더 큰 권위를 가진다.) → 비언어가 우세하다는 글과 반대예요.

② A discrepancy between verbal assurances and nonverbal indicators prompts listeners to doubt the speaker's sincerity. (말로 한 다짐과 비언어 신호가 어긋나면 듣는 사람은 말하는 사람의 진심을 의심한다.) → 억지 미소, 찡그린 얼굴 예와 일치해요.

③ Adults find it effortless to manipulate others using speech regardless of their body language. (어른들은 몸짓과 상관없이 말로 남을 쉽게 조종한다.) → 비언어가 맞지 않으면 실패한다고 했어요.

④ Inconsistent messages have minimal impact on how credible a speaker appears to others. (일치하지 않는 메시지는 말하는 사람의 신뢰도에 거의 영향이 없다.) → 말을 덜 믿게 된다고 했으니 반대예요.

⑤ A forced smile or averted gaze rarely diminishes the credibility of a flattering verbal remark. (억지 미소나 피하는 시선은 듣기 좋은 말의 신뢰도를 거의 떨어뜨리지 않는다.) → 오히려 덜 믿게 된다고 했어요.
