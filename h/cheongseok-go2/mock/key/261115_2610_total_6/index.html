Imagine you are lending to somebody at an interest rate of 10 percent per year for five years. This rate tells us that the money lent has a cost that reflects __________. The longer the term of the loan, the higher the chance you won’t be paid back because it is further into the future ― which by definition is unknowable ― and the longer you will have to wait before spending the money yourself. 문제 본문 다시 보기
Research on cephalopod neurobiology suggests that an octopus possesses roughly five hundred million neurons. What makes this system unusual is where many of those neurons are located. More than half are distributed throughout the arms rather than concentrated entirely in the central brain. Each arm contains networks of nerve cells capable of controlling complex movements and responding to sensory input locally. This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. (A)_______, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system that interact with the environment directly. The suckers lining each arm play an important role in this process. These structures provide strong grip but also detect chemical signals on surfaces they contact. Marine biologists studying octopus behavior have shown that this allows the animal to gather chemical information through touch. (B)_______, an octopus can effectively taste what it touches.
정답: ①

보기와 우리말 뜻

① Instead - In fact (대신 - 실제로) → 둘 다 흐름에 맞아요.

② Hence - In fact (그러므로 - 실제로) → (A)는 "뇌가 아니라 팔이" 하는 대조라 Instead(대신)가 더 맞아요.

③ Instead - On the contrary (대신 - 그와 반대로) → (B)는 앞 내용을 쉽게 다시 말하는 자리라 반대 연결어는 틀려요.

④ Similarly - In fact (마찬가지로 - 실제로) → (A)는 비슷한 내용 추가가 아니에요.

⑤ Instead - For example (대신 - 예를 들어) → (B)는 예시가 아니라 앞 내용을 다시 정리한 말이에요.

답의 근거 (본문)

This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. (이것은 많은 행동이 뇌 자체의 자세한 지시를 필요로 하지 않는다는 뜻이에요.)

Instead, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system that interact with the environment directly. (대신 팔은 환경과 직접 상호 작용하는, 아주 유능한 신경계의 연장선으로 작동해요.)

In practical terms, an octopus can effectively taste what it touches. (실제로 말하면, 문어는 자기가 만지는 것을 사실상 맛볼 수 있어요.)

쉬운 풀이

(A): 앞 문장은 많은 행동이 뇌의 지시가 "필요 없다"고 해요.

뇌 대신 팔이 직접 일한다는 대조라 Instead(대신)가 맞아요.

원래 글은 In practical terms(실제로 말하면)이고, 같은 역할로 In fact(실제로)가 맞아요.

그래서 정답은 ①이에요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Human development is now increasingly understood as an ongoing, flexible, and evolving process rather than a fixed sequence of stages. At the core of this perspective lies the concept of plasticity. Plasticity refers to the human capacity to change, adapt, and reorganize cognitive, emotional, and behavioural patterns in response to life experiences, environmental demands, and biological changes. It highlights that individuals are not merely passive recipients of development but active agents capable of growth throughout their lives. In earlier views, development was believed to occur mainly during childhood, with adulthood seen as a period of stability and old age as a phase of decline. The concept of plasticity challenges this rigid outlook by emphasizing that development continues across the entire lifespan. Although the extent of change may vary at different stages, every phase of life allows room for adaptation and transformation. 정답: ③

④ declining - maintain (쇠퇴하는 - 유지하다) → 둘 다 글과 반대예요.

⑤ lasting - preserve (지속되는 - 보존하다) → (B)의 ‘그대로 보존하다’는 변화와 반대예요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
A simple truth that designers must embrace in order to create more effective products and experiences is the fact that we are not the user. We’re much too close to what we build to objectively see it and understand how users will interact with it. The best user interfaces are those that have been shaped by observations of real users and their interactions with them. So how can designers shape their work to be more effective? An incredibly effective way is with usability testing. The goal of this method is to uncover problems in a design, discover existing opportunities, and learn more about the behaviors and preferences of users by asking participants to engage in realistic activities. 이러한 특화 덕분에 돌고래의 뇌는 반향 정보를 순차적으로가 아니라 동시에(병렬로) 처리할 수 있다.

This parallel processing reduces the cognitive load associated with echolocation, allowing dolphins to react quickly to changes in their environment.
이러한 병렬 처리는 반향정위와 관련된 인지적 부담을 줄여 돌고래가 환경 변화에 빠르게 반응할 수 있게 한다.

지루함은 안전할 때 누리는 사치, 자기 성찰과 성장의 기회

문제: 지루함을 방치하면 우울, 절망, 짜증 같은 해로운 감정이 생겨요
▶
원인: 재미있는 활동에만 기대면 의존하게 되고, 한가한 시간엔 괴로운 생각이 늘어요
▶
전환: 지루함을 낭비가 아니라 자기 성찰과 향상의 시간으로 봐요
▶
근거: 지루함은 위험이 없는 안전한 곳에서만 누리는 사치예요
▶
결론: 한가한 순간을 깊은 성찰과 성장의 기회로 써야 해요

원문 핵심 문장

The balance is to not see it as idle time, wasted time, or boredom but rather as a perfect time for self-improvement, self-identification, and positive reflection time.
균형은 그것을 한가한 시간, 낭비된 시간, 또는 지루함으로 보지 않고, 오히려 자기 계발, 자기 정체성 확인, 긍정적인 성찰을 위한 완벽한 시간으로 보는 것이다.

Boredom can be perceived as a luxury that typically manifests in safe and inactive situations, providing an occasion to appreciate and comprehend the environment.
지루함은 보통 안전하고 활동이 없는 상황에서 나타나는 사치로 여겨질 수 있으며, 환경을 감상하고 이해할 기회를 제공한다.

Rather than viewing boredom as unproductive, it should be regarded as an opportunity for personal development and constructive contemplation.
지루함을 비생산적인 것으로 보기보다는 개인적 성장과 건설적인 사유의 기회로 여겨야 한다.
