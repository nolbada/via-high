Reappraisal — changing the way one thinks about emotional situations — might present a particularly effective avenue to regulating emotions because it transforms emotions at their root. Take, for example, someone who is going through a divorce. This is often an adverse event, and yet, it can be construed in different ways. One might view it as an overpowering loss that will mark one’s family for life. Alternatively, one might see it as something that is painful but that might lead to positive outcomes in the long run, such as improved relationships. As a result of these different construals, one might feel different emotions. The first construal may bring intense sadness and anger. The second construal might also bring sadness and anger but perhaps at lower levels. Crucially, it might also bring some positive emotions, such as hope, strength, or gratitude. This example illustrates how reappraisal might powerfully transform short-term emotional responses. When repeated over time, these short-term emotional consequences of reappraisal might accumulate to shape people’s psychological health. Thereby, reappraisal might be a key to resilience. *resilience: 회복 탄력성 **construe: 해석하다

Dolphins’ brains have evolved specialized areas dedicated to echolocation, allowing them to process acoustic information more quickly and with less energy. One key adaptation is the structure of the dolphin’s auditory pathway. The signals from the ears are rapidly transmitted to the auditory cortex, the brain region responsible for processing sound. However, unlike many other mammals, dolphins have a highly specialized auditory cortex with distinct areas dedicated to processing different aspects of echolocation signals. Some areas may be responsible for analyzing the timing of echoes, while others focus on the frequency content or the intensity of the sound. This specialization allows the dolphin’s brain to process echo information in parallel, rather than sequentially. Imagine a factory assembly line, where different workers are responsible for different tasks. By dividing the work among specialized areas, the brain can process information much faster and more efficiently. This parallel processing ____, allowing dolphins to react quickly to changes in their environment.
정답: ①

보기와 우리말 뜻

① lightens the mental effort required to interpret echolocation data (반향 정위 데이터를 해석하는 데 필요한 정신적 노력을 덜어 준다) → 인지적 부담이 줄어든다는 뜻이에요.

② increases the complexity of signals that dolphins must analyze at once (돌고래가 한꺼번에 분석할 신호의 복잡성을 높인다) → 반응이 빨라진다는 글과 맞지 않아요.

③ forces dolphins to rely more heavily on vision than on acoustic sensing (돌고래가 음향 감지보다 시각에 더 의존하게 만든다) → 시각 얘기는 없어요.

④ makes it harder for dolphins to distinguish between overlapping echo signals (겹치는 반향 신호를 구별하기 더 어렵게 만든다) → 효율적으로 처리한다는 글과 반대예요.

⑤ slows down information transfer between the auditory cortex and other regions (청각 피질과 다른 영역 사이의 정보 전달을 느리게 한다) → 빠르게 처리한다는 글과 반대예요.

답의 근거 (본문)

This specialization allows the dolphin’s brain to process echo information in parallel, rather than sequentially. (이런 특화 덕분에 돌고래의 뇌는 반향 정보를 순차적이 아니라 동시에 처리할 수 있어요.)

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

② 복잡성이 높아지면 빠르고 효율적이라는 앞 내용과 맞지 않아요.

③ 시각 얘기는 글에 없어요.

④ 구별이 어려워지면 빠르게 반응하기 힘들어요. 글과 반대예요.

⑤ 전달이 느려지면 빠르게 반응한다는 말과 반대예요.

① (B)가 틀려요. reassuring(안심시키는) 생각이면 악마의 작업장이라는 말과 안 맞아요.

② (A)(B)(C)가 모두 반대 뜻이에요. 의존하는 게 아니라 독립이라면 문제가 안 생겨요.

④ (A)가 틀려요. independent of(~에서 독립한)이면 활동에 기대는 게 아니라서 부정적 감정이 생긴다는 흐름이 안 맞아요.

⑤ (C)가 틀려요. destructive(파괴적인)는 '발전의 기회'와 이어지지 않아요.

시험 포인트

괄호 어휘는 앞뒤 문장이 좋은 흐름인지 나쁜 흐름인지부터 정해요. dependent / independent, constructive / destructive처럼 반의어 짝은 모고에서 자주 나와서 뜻을 정확히 외워 둬요.
