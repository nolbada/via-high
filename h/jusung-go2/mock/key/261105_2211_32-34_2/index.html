However, there are unexpected characteristics when it comes to such infection in the market. Infection can occur even without any contact. ② to buy는 형용사 expensive(비싼)를 꾸며 '사기에 비싼'으로 해석돼요. 명사를 꾸미는 쓰임이 아니라서 달라요.

③ to engage는 앞의 가짜 주어 It 대신 '참여시키는 것'이라는 진짜 주어 노릇을 해요. 명사를 꾸미는 쓰임이 아니라서 달라요.

⑤ to discover는 '발견하고 (놀랐다)'처럼 놀란 이유를 나타내요. 명사를 꾸미는 쓰임이 아니라서 달라요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
The connectedness of the global economic market makes it vulnerable to potential “infection.” A financial failure can make its way from borrowers to banks to insurers, spreading like a flu. However, there are unexpected characteristics when it comes to such infection in the market. Infection can occur even without any contact. A bank might become insolvent even without having any of its investments fail. Fear and uncertainty can be damaging to financial markets, just as cascading failures due to bad investments. If we all woke up tomorrow and believed that Bank X would be insolvent, then it would become insolvent. In fact, it would be enough for us to fear that others believed that Bank X was going to fail, or just to fear our collective fear! We might all even know that Bank X was well-managed with healthy investments, but if we expected others ㉠(pull) their money out, then we would fear ㉡(be) the last to pull our money out. Financial distress can be self-fulfilling and is a particularly troublesome aspect of financial markets. *insolvent: 지급 불능의, 파산한 **cascading: 연속된
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

pull - to be → ㉠ pull=빼다(동사원형) / ㉡ to be=~가 되는 것(be ~이다, to부정사)

pulling - been → ㉠ pulling=빼는 것(pull 빼다, -ing) / ㉡ been=~였던(be ~이다, 과거분사)

to pull - been → ㉠ to pull=빼도록(pull 빼다, to부정사) / ㉡ been=~였던(be ~이다, 과거분사)

to pull - being → ㉠ to pull=빼도록(pull 빼다, to부정사) / ㉡ being=~가 되는 것(be ~이다, -ing)

pulling - being → ㉠ pulling=빼는 것(pull 빼다, -ing) / ㉡ being=~가 되는 것(be ~이다, -ing)

답의 근거 (본문)

We might all even know that Bank X was well-managed with healthy investments, but if we expected others ㉠(pull) their money out, then we would fear ㉡(be) the last to pull our money out. (우리 모두 X 은행이 건전한 투자로 잘 운영되고 있다는 걸 알더라도, 다른 사람들이 돈을 빼 갈 거라고(to pull) 예상한다면, 우리는 돈을 빼는 마지막 사람이 되는 것(being)을 두려워할 거예요.)

쉬운 풀이

㉠ expect(예상하다)는 'expect + 사람 + to 동사원형' 모양으로 써서 '(사람)이 ~할 거라고 예상하다'라는 뜻이 돼요. 그래서 expected others to pull(다른 사람들이 돈을 뺄 거라고 예상했다)이 맞아요. ㉡ fear(두려워하다) 뒤에 '~가 되는 것'이 오도록 being(be ~이다 → ~가 되는 것)을 써서 fear being the last(마지막 사람이 되는 것을 두려워하다)가 돼요. 그래서 to pull - being인 ④가 정답이에요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① ㉠ expect 뒤에는 동사원형 pull이 아니라 to pull이 와야 해서 틀려요.

② ㉠ expect 뒤에는 pulling이 아니라 to pull이 와야 하고, ㉡ been은 have 없이 혼자 쓸 수 없어요.

③ ㉠ to pull은 맞지만, ㉡ been은 have 없이 혼자 쓸 수 없어서 틀려요.

⑤ ㉡ being은 맞지만, ㉠ expect 뒤에는 pulling이 아니라 to pull이 와야 해요.

쉬운 풀이

make its way는 '(어디로) 나아가다, 퍼져 가다'라는 뜻이에요. 본문에서 금융 실패가 돈을 빌린 사람에서 은행, 보험사로 '퍼져 간다'는 말이에요. 그런데 change(바꾸다, 변하다)로 바꾸면 '퍼져 간다'는 뜻이 사라져요. 그래서 ①은 바꿔 쓸 수 없어요.

③ occur와 happen은 둘 다 '일어나다'라서 바꿔 쓸 수 있어요.

④ due to와 owing to는 둘 다 '~ 때문에'라서 바꿔 쓸 수 있어요.

⑤ well-managed(잘 관리되는)와 well-run(잘 운영되는)은 같은 뜻이라 바꿔 쓸 수 있어요.

Observational studies of humans cannot be properly controlled. Humans live different lifestyles and in different environments. Thus, they are insufficiently homogeneous to be suitable experimental subjects. (A) For example, Taubes argued that since many people who drink also smoke, researchers have difficulty determining the link between alcohol consumption and cancer. Similarly, researchers in the famous Framingham study identified a significant correlation between coffee drinking and coronary heart disease. (B) These confounding factors undermine our ability to draw sound causal conclusions from human epidemiological surveys. Confounding factors are variables (known or unknown) that make it difficult for epidemiologists to isolate the effects of the specific variable being studied. (C) However, most of this correlation disappeared once researchers corrected for the fact that many coffee drinkers also smoke. If the confounding factors are known, it is often possible to correct for them.
