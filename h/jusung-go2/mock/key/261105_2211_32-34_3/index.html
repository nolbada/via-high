위 글의 괄호 ㉢, ㉣, ㉤에서 어법상 알맞은 말로 바르게 연결된 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
The connectedness of the global economic market makes it vulnerable to potential “infection.” A financial failure can make its way from borrowers to banks to insurers, spreading like a flu. However, there are unexpected characteristics when it comes to such infection in the market. Infection can occur even without any contact. A bank might become insolvent even without having any of its investments fail. Fear and uncertainty can be damaging to financial markets, just as cascading failures due to bad investments. If we all woke up tomorrow and believed ㉢(that / what) Bank X would be insolvent, then it would ㉣(become / have become) insolvent. In fact, it would be enough for us to fear that others believed ㉢(that / what) Bank X was going to fail, or just ㉤(fearing / to fear) our collective fear! We might all even know that Bank X was well-managed with healthy investments, but if we expected others to pull their money out, then we would fear being the last to pull our money out. Financial distress can be self-fulfilling and is a particularly troublesome aspect of financial markets. *insolvent: 지급 불능의, 파산한 **cascading: 연속된
정답: ②

보기와 우리말 뜻

that - become - fearing → ㉢ that=~라는 것 / ㉣ become=~이 되다 / ㉤ fearing=두려워하는 것(fear=두려워하다)

that - become - to fear → ㉢ that=~라는 것 / ㉣ become=~이 되다 / ㉤ to fear=두려워하는 것(fear=두려워하다)

that - have become - fearing → ㉢ that=~라는 것 / ㉣ have become=~이 되었다(become=~이 되다) / ㉤ fearing=두려워하는 것(fear=두려워하다)

what - become - to fear → ㉢ what=~하는 것 / ㉣ become=~이 되다 / ㉤ to fear=두려워하는 것(fear=두려워하다)

what - have become - to fear → ㉢ what=~하는 것 / ㉣ have become=~이 되었다(become=~이 되다) / ㉤ to fear=두려워하는 것(fear=두려워하다)

답의 근거 (본문)

If we all woke up tomorrow and believed ㉢(that / what) Bank X would be insolvent, then it would ㉣(become / have become) insolvent. (만약 우리 모두가 내일 일어나서 X 은행이 지급 불능이 될 거라고(that) 믿는다면, 그 은행은 지급 불능이 될(become) 거예요.)

In fact, it would be enough for us to fear that others believed ㉢(that / what) Bank X was going to fail, or just ㉤(fearing / to fear) our collective fear! (사실, 우리가 다른 사람들이 X 은행이 망할 거라고(that) 믿는다는 것을 두려워하거나, 그저 우리의 집단적인 두려움을 두려워하는 것(to fear)만으로도 충분할 거예요!)

쉬운 풀이

㉢ believed 뒤에 'Bank X would be insolvent'처럼 빠진 것이 없는 완전한 문장이 오니까 that(~라는 것)이 맞아요. what은 뒤 문장에 빠진 것이 있을 때 써요. ㉣ 'If we all woke up ~'은 지금·앞날을 상상하는 가정법 과거라서 'would+동사원형'인 would become(become=~이 되다)이 맞아요. ㉤ 'for us to fear ~, or just to fear ~'처럼 앞의 to fear와 or로 나란히 이어지니까 to fear(fear=두려워하다)예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① ㉢·㉣은 맞지만 ㉤ fearing은 틀려요. or 앞의 to fear와 모양을 맞춰 to fear를 써야 해요.

③ ㉣ have become은 '이미 ~이 되었을 것'이라는 과거 상상이라 틀리고, ㉤ fearing도 to fear로 써야 해요.

④ ㉢ what은 틀려요. 뒤에 완전한 문장이 오니까 that을 써야 해요.

⑤ ㉢ what은 that으로, ㉣ have become은 become으로 써야 해서 틀려요.

Confounding factors are variables (known or unknown) that make it difficult for epidemiologists to isolate the effects of the specific variable being studied. For example, Taubes argued that since many people who drink also smoke, researchers have difficulty determining the link between alcohol consumption and cancer. Similarly, researchers in the famous Framingham study identified a significant correlation between coffee drinking and coronary heart disease. However, most of this correlation disappeared once researchers corrected for the fact that many coffee drinkers also smoke. If the confounding factors are known, it is often possible to correct for them.
