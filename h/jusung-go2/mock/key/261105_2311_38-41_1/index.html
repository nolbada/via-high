다음 글의 요지로 가장 적절한 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Introverted leaders do have to overcome the strong cultural presumption that extroverts are more effective leaders. Although the population splits into almost equal parts between introverts and extroverts, more than 96 percent of managers and executives are extroverted. In a study done in 2006, 65 percent of senior corporate executives viewed introversion as a barrier to leadership. We must reexamine this stereotype, however, as it doesn’t always hold true. Regent University found that a desire to be of service to others and to empower them to grow, which is more common among introverts than extroverts, is a key factor in becoming a leader and retaining leadership. So-called servant leadership, dating back to ancient philosophical literature, adheres to the belief that a company’s goals are best achieved by helping workers or customers achieve their goals. Such leaders do not seek attention but rather want to shine a light on others’ wins and achievements; servant leadership requires humility, but that humility ultimately pays off. * humility: 겸손
정답: ⑤

보기와 우리말 뜻

Introverted individuals, when assuming leadership roles, tend to gravitate towards servant leadership. (내향적인 사람은 리더가 되면 서번트 리더십 쪽으로 끌리는 경향이 있다.)

Leadership is not determined by individual traits but can be achieved through effort and dedication. (리더십은 개인의 성향으로 정해지지 않고 노력과 헌신으로 얻을 수 있다.)

Individuals with extroverted tendencies can become leaders who are capable of assisting others in their success. (외향적인 사람은 남의 성공을 도울 수 있는 리더가 될 수 있다.)

Servant leadership is a fitting leadership style that attracts people's attention and fosters the success of the team. (서번트 리더십은 사람들의 관심을 끌고 팀의 성공을 키우는 알맞은 리더십이다.)

The stereotype that individuals with extroverted tendencies are more likely to become competent leaders should be reconsidered. (외향적인 사람이 유능한 리더가 될 가능성이 더 높다는 고정관념은 다시 생각해 봐야 한다.)

답의 근거 (본문)

We must reexamine this stereotype, however, as it doesn’t always hold true. (하지만 이 고정관념은 늘 맞는 게 아니므로 우리는 그것을 다시 살펴봐야 해요.)

Regent University found that a desire to be of service to others and to empower them to grow, which is more common among introverts than extroverts, is a key factor in becoming a leader and retaining leadership. (Regent 대학은 남에게 도움이 되고 그들이 성장하도록 힘을 주려는 마음이 외향인보다 내향인에게 더 흔하며, 리더가 되고 리더십을 지키는 핵심 요소라는 것을 알아냈어요.)

쉬운 풀이

글은 '외향인이 더 좋은 리더'라는 생각을 먼저 소개한 뒤, however(하지만)로 이 고정관념을 다시 살펴봐야 한다고 말해요. 그리고 리더의 핵심 자질인 남을 돕고 성장시키려는 마음이 오히려 내향인에게 더 흔하다는 연구를 근거로 들어요. 그래서 글쓴이가 가장 하고 싶은 말은 ⑤ '외향인이 더 유능한 리더라는 고정관념은 다시 생각해야 한다'예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 내향인이 서번트 리더십을 '고르는 경향'이 있다고는 말하지 않았어요. 서번트 리더십은 근거로 든 예일 뿐이에요.

② 노력과 헌신으로 리더십을 얻는다는 내용은 글에 없어요.

③ 글은 내향인의 장점을 말하는 글이지, 외향인이 남을 돕는 리더가 될 수 있다는 글이 아니에요.

④ 서번트 리더는 관심을 받으려 하지 않는다(do not seek attention)고 했으니 반대예요.

📌 2311-38번 글 내용 정리

사람들은 흔히 외향적인 사람이 더 좋은 리더라고 생각해요. 실제로 관리자의 96퍼센트 이상이 외향적이고, 임원의 65퍼센트는 내향성을 걸림돌로 봤어요. 하지만 남을 돕고 성장시키려는 마음은 내향인에게 더 흔하고, 이것이 리더가 되는 핵심이에요. 이런 서번트 리더십은 겸손을 필요로 하지만 결국 좋은 결과로 돌아와요.

In Western society, many music performance settings make a clear distinction between performers and audience members: the performers are the “doers” and those in the audience take a decidedly passive role. The performance space itself may further reinforce the distinction with a physical separation between the stage and audience seating. Perhaps because this distinction is so common, audiences seem to greatly value opportunities to have special “access” to performers that affords understanding about performers’ style of music. Some performing musicians have won great approval by regularly incorporating “audience participation” into their concerts. Whether by leading a sing-along activity or teaching a rhythm to be clapped at certain points, including audience members in the music making can boost the level of engagement and enjoyment for all involved. Performers who are uncomfortable leading audience participation can still connect with the audience simply by giving a special glimpse of the performer perspective. It is quite common in classical music to provide audiences with program notes. Typically, this text in a program gives background information about pieces of music being performed and perhaps biographical information about historically significant composers. What may be of more interest to audience members is background information about the very performers who are onstage, including an explanation of why they have chosen the music they are presenting. Such insight can make audience members feel closer to the musicians onstage, both metaphorically and emotionally. This connection will likely enhance the expressive and communicative experience.
