윗글의 괄호 ㉠, ㉡, ㉢에서 어법상 알맞은 말로 바르게 연결된 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
The arts and aesthetics offer emotional connection to the full range of human experience. “The arts can be more than just sugar on the tongue,” Anjan Chatterjee, a professor at the University of Pennsylvania, says. “In art, when there’s something challenging, which can also be uncomfortable, this discomfort, if we’re willing to ㉠(engage / engaging) with it, offers the possibility of some change, some transformation. That can also be a powerful aesthetic experience.” The arts, in this way, become vehicles to contend with ideas and concepts that are difficult and uncomfortable otherwise. When Picasso painted his masterpiece Guernica in 1937, he captured the heartbreaking and cruel nature of war, and offered the world a way to consider the universal suffering caused by the Spanish Civil War. When Lorraine Hansberry wrote her play A Raisin in the Sun, she gave us a powerful story of people ㉡(struggling / struggled) with racism, discrimination, and the pursuit of the American dream while also ㉢(offering / offered) a touching portrait of family life.
정답: ①

보기와 우리말 뜻

답의 근거 (본문)

“In art, when there’s something challenging, which can also be uncomfortable, this discomfort, if we’re willing to ㉠(engage / engaging) with it, offers the possibility of some change, some transformation. (“예술에서 불편할 수도 있는 도전적인 무언가가 있을 때, 이 불편함은, 우리가 기꺼이 그것과 마주한다면(engage), 어떤 변화, 어떤 탈바꿈의 가능성을 제공해요.)

When Lorraine Hansberry wrote her play A Raisin in the Sun, she gave us a powerful story of people ㉡(struggling / struggled) with racism, discrimination, and the pursuit of the American dream while also ㉢(offering / offered) a touching portrait of family life. (Lorraine Hansberry가 희곡 A Raisin in the Sun을 썼을 때, 그녀는 인종 차별, 차별, 아메리칸 드림의 추구와 힘겹게 싸우는(struggling) 사람들의 강렬한 이야기를 우리에게 주면서, 가족생활의 감동적인 모습도 보여 주었어요(offering).)

쉬운 풀이

㉠ 'be willing to(기꺼이 ~하다)' 뒤에는 동사원형이 오니 engage(마주하다)가 맞아요. ㉡ people(사람들)을 뒤에서 꾸미는 자리예요. 사람들이 스스로 싸우는 것이니 능동의 struggling(struggle=힘겹게 싸우다)이 맞아요. ㉢ while 뒤에 주어가 없으니 '~하면서'라는 뜻의 offering(offer=보여 주다)이 맞아요. 그래서 ①이에요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

② ㉠ engage: 맞아요. ㉡ struggled: 사람들이 스스로 싸우는 것이니 능동 struggling이어야 해요. struggle은 '~을 당하다'로 쓰지 않아요. ㉢ offering: 맞아요.

③ ㉠ engaging: be willing to 뒤에는 동사원형 engage가 와야 해요. ㉡ struggling: 맞아요. ㉢ offering: 맞아요.

④ ㉠ engage: 맞아요. ㉡ struggling: 맞아요. ㉢ offered: while 뒤에 주어가 없으니 '~하면서'의 offering이어야 해요.

⑤ ㉠ engaging: 동사원형 engage여야 해요. ㉡ struggled: 능동 struggling이어야 해요. ㉢ offered: while 뒤에 주어가 없으니 offering이어야 해요.

The arts and aesthetics offer emotional connection to the full range of human experience. “The arts can be more than just sugar on the tongue,” Anjan Chatterjee, a professor at the University of Pennsylvania, says. “In art, when there’s something challenging, ⓐwhich can also be uncomfortable, this discomfort, if we’re willing to engage with it, offers the possibility of some change, some transformation. That can also be a powerful aesthetic experience.” The arts, in this way, ⓑbecome vehicles to contend with ideas and concepts that are difficult and uncomfortable otherwise. When Picasso painted his masterpiece Guernica in 1937, he captured the ⓒheartbreaking and cruel nature of war, and offered the world a way to consider the universal suffering ⓓwas caused by the Spanish Civil War. 정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

ⓐ which=그런데 그것은(앞말을 보충하는 관계대명사)

ⓑ become=~이 되다

ⓒ heartbreaking=가슴 아프게 하는

ⓓ was caused(cause=일으키다)=일으켜졌다

답의 근거 (본문)

When Picasso painted his masterpiece Guernica in 1937, he captured the ⓒheartbreaking and cruel nature of war, and offered the world a way to consider the universal suffering ⓓwas caused by the Spanish Civil War. (피카소가 1937년에 걸작 게르니카를 그렸을 때, 그는 전쟁의 가슴 아프고(heartbreaking) 잔인한 본모습을 담았고, 스페인 내전에 의해 일으켜진(caused) 모두의 고통을 생각해 볼 방법을 세상에 주었어요.)

“In art, when there’s something challenging, ⓐwhich can also be uncomfortable, this discomfort, if we’re willing to engage with it, offers the possibility of some change, some transformation. (“예술에서 도전적인 무언가가 있을 때, 그런데 그것은(which) 불편할 수도 있는데, 이 불편함은, 우리가 기꺼이 그것과 마주한다면, 어떤 변화, 어떤 탈바꿈의 가능성을 제공해요.)

The arts, in this way, ⓑbecome vehicles to contend with ideas and concepts that are difficult and uncomfortable otherwise. (이런 식으로 예술은, 그렇지 않으면 어렵고 불편했을 생각과 개념에 맞서 싸울 수단이 돼요(become).)

쉬운 풀이

ⓓ 이 부분의 동사는 이미 offered(offer=주다)가 있어요. the universal suffering(모두의 고통)은 그 뒤에 따라오는 말이고, '스페인 내전에 의해 일으켜진' 고통이라고 뒤에서 꾸며 주는 거예요. 꾸며 주는 말에는 was가 붙으면 안 되니 caused(cause=일으키다)만 써야 해요. 그래서 어색한 것은 ④예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

② ⓑ 주어 The arts(예술들)가 여러 개라서 s 없는 become(되다)이 맞아요.

⑤ ⓔ 가족생활의 모습이 사람을 감동시키는 것이라 touching(감동적인)이 맞아요.

⑤ (A) embrace는 맞지만 (B) permanence(영원히 그대로)는 변화와 반대예요.

답의 근거 (본문)

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 틀린 것은 ⓑ sought, ⓒ what, ⓔ was made, ⓕ asking 4개라서 2개는 너무 적어요.

② 틀린 것이 4개라서 3개는 하나가 모자라요.

④ ⓐ that(장치를 꾸미는 말), ⓓ carrying(instead of 뒤라 -ing), ⓖ disguised(바람잡이가 '변장한')는 맞으니 5개는 너무 많아요.

⑤ 맞는 것이 ⓐ, ⓓ, ⓖ 3개 있으니 6개는 너무 많아요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Sylvan Goldman invented the shopping cart and introduced it in his stores in 1937. It was an excellent device that would make it easy for shoppers to buy as much as they wanted without getting tired or seeking others’ help. (A) It was only a few elderly shoppers who used them. That made the carts even less attractive to the majority of the shoppers. Then Goldman hit upon an idea. He hired several models, men and women, of different ages and asked them to wheel the carts in the store and shop. (B) A young woman employee standing near the entrance told the regular shoppers, ‘Look, everyone is using the carts. Why don’t you?’ That was the turning point. A few shills disguised as regular shoppers easily accomplished what logic, explanations, and advertisements failed to do. Within a few weeks shoppers readily accepted those carts. (C) But Goldman discovered that in spite of his repeated advertisements and explanations, he could not persuade his shoppers to use the wheeled carts. Men were reluctant because they thought they would appear weak if they pushed such carts instead of carrying their shopping. Women wouldn’t touch them because the carts reminded them of baby carriages. *shill: 바람잡이
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

(A)-(C)-(B) (노인만 씀·모델 고용 → 광고에도 손님들이 꺼림 → 직원의 한마디로 카트 정착)

(B)-(A)-(C) (카트 정착 → 노인만 씀·모델 고용 → 손님들이 꺼림)

(B)-(C)-(A) (카트 정착 → 손님들이 꺼림 → 노인만 씀·모델 고용)

(C)-(A)-(B) (광고에도 손님들이 꺼림 → 노인만 씀·모델 고용 → 직원의 한마디로 카트 정착)

(C)-(B)-(A) (손님들이 꺼림 → 카트 정착 → 노인만 씀·모델 고용)

답의 근거 (본문)

But Goldman discovered that in spite of his repeated advertisements and explanations, he could not persuade his shoppers to use the wheeled carts. (하지만 Goldman은 거듭된 광고와 설명에도 불구하고 손님들이 바퀴 달린 카트를 쓰도록 설득할 수 없다는 것을 알게 되었어요.)

Then Goldman hit upon an idea. (그때 Goldman이 한 가지 아이디어를 떠올렸어요.)

That was the turning point. (그것이 전환점이었어요.)

쉬운 풀이

주어진 글은 카트가 훌륭한 장치였다고 끝나요. 'But(하지만)'으로 손님들이 안 썼다는 문제를 꺼내는 (C)가 먼저예요. (C) 끝에서 남자·여자가 꺼린 이유가 나오고, (A)에서 노인들만 썼다는 이유가 더해진 뒤 Goldman이 모델을 고용해요. 그 결과로 (B)에서 직원의 한마디가 전환점이 되어 손님들이 카트를 받아들여요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① (A)의 them(카트)과 노인 이야기는 (C)의 문제 제기 뒤에 와야 해요.

② 결과 (B)가 맨 앞에 올 수 없어요.

③ 결과 (B)가 맨 앞에 올 수 없어요.

⑤ (B)의 직원과 모델 이야기는 (A)에서 모델을 고용한 뒤에 와야 해요.

다음 글의 내용을 한 문장으로 요약하고자 한다. 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Sylvan Goldman invented the shopping cart and introduced it in his stores in 1937. It was an excellent device that would make it easy for shoppers to buy as much as they wanted without getting tired or seeking others’ help. But Goldman discovered that in spite of his repeated advertisements and explanations, he could not persuade his shoppers to use the wheeled carts. Men were reluctant because they thought they would appear weak if they pushed such carts instead of carrying their shopping. Women wouldn’t touch them because the carts reminded them of baby carriages. It was only a few elderly shoppers who used them. That made the carts even less attractive to the majority of the shoppers. Then Goldman hit upon an idea. He hired several models, men and women, of different ages and asked them to wheel the carts in the store and shop. A young woman employee standing near the entrance told the regular shoppers, ‘Look, everyone is using the carts. Why don’t you?’ That was the turning point. A few shills disguised as regular shoppers easily accomplished what logic, explanations, and advertisements failed to do. Within a few weeks shoppers readily accepted those carts. * shill: 바람잡이 The shopping cart, which is great but not ____(A)____ by people, has become ____(B)____ by effectively using a few shills.
정답: ④

보기와 우리말 뜻

convenient – adopted (편리한 – 받아들여진)

used – rejected (사용되는 – 거절당한)

practical – ignored (실용적인 – 무시당한)

used – accepted (사용되는 – 받아들여진)

helpful – refused (도움이 되는 – 거부당한)

답의 근거 (본문)

It was only a few elderly shoppers who used them. (카트를 쓴 사람은 오직 몇몇 나이 든 손님들뿐이었어요.)

Within a few weeks shoppers readily accepted those carts. (몇 주 만에 쇼핑객들은 그 카트를 기꺼이 받아들였어요.)

쉬운 풀이

(A) 카트는 훌륭했지만 사람들이 쓰지 않았으니 'not used by people(사람들에게 사용되지 않는)'이 맞아요. (B) 바람잡이 덕분에 손님들이 카트를 받아들였으니 accepted(받아들여진)가 맞아요. 완성 문장은 'The shopping cart, which is great but not used by people, has become accepted by effectively using a few shills. (훌륭하지만 사람들이 쓰지 않던 쇼핑 카트는 바람잡이 몇 명을 잘 활용해서 받아들여지게 되었다.)'예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① (A) '사람들에 의해 편리하지 않다'는 말이 어색하고, 글은 카트가 편리했다고 해요.

② (A)는 맞지만 (B) 거절당했다는 건 결과와 반대예요.

③ (A) 카트는 실용적이었고, (B) 무시당했다는 것도 결과와 반대예요.

⑤ (A) 카트는 도움이 되는 도구였고, (B) 거부당했다는 것도 결과와 반대예요.
