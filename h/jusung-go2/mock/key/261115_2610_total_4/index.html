The longer the term of the loan, the higher the chance you won’t be paid back because it is further into the future ― which by definition is unknowable ― and the longer you will have to wait before spending the money yourself. 문제 본문 다시 보기
Research on cephalopod neurobiology suggests that an octopus possesses roughly five hundred million neurons. What makes this system unusual is where many of those neurons are located. More than half are distributed throughout the arms rather than concentrated entirely in the central brain. Each arm contains networks of nerve cells capable of controlling complex movements and responding to sensory input locally. ①This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. ②Instead, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system that interact with the environment directly. ③The existence of such an excellent neural network has made the octopus the species best adapted to the marine environment. ④The suckers lining each arm play an important role in this process, so these structures provide strong grip but also detect chemical signals on surfaces they contact. ⑤Marine biologists studying octopus behavior have shown that this allows the animal to gather chemical information through touch, which means, in practical terms, an octopus can effectively taste what it touches.
정답: ③

보기와 우리말 뜻

① This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. (많은 행동이 뇌의 자세한 지시를 필요로 하지 않아요.) → 흐름에 맞아요.

② Instead, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system ... (대신 팔은 신경계의 연장선으로 작동해요 ...) → 흐름에 맞아요.

③ The existence of such an excellent neural network has made the octopus the species best adapted to the marine environment. (이런 뛰어난 신경망 덕분에 문어는 바다 환경에 가장 잘 적응한 종이 되었어요.) → "가장 잘 적응한 종" 비교 이야기라 관계없어요.

④ The suckers lining each arm play an important role in this process, ... (각 팔의 빨판이 이 과정에서 중요한 역할을 해요 ...) → 흐름에 맞아요.

⑤ Marine biologists ... an octopus can effectively taste what it touches. (해양 생물학자들은 ... 문어가 만지는 것을 맛볼 수 있다고 보여 줬어요.) → 결론이라 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

Instead, the arms function as highly capable extensions of the nervous system that interact with the environment directly. (대신 팔은 환경과 직접 상호 작용하는, 아주 유능한 신경계의 연장선으로 작동해요.)

These structures provide strong grip but also detect chemical signals on surfaces they contact. (이 구조(빨판)는 강하게 잡아 줄 뿐 아니라, 닿는 표면의 화학 신호도 감지해요.)

쉬운 풀이

글은 문어 팔의 신경망이 어떻게 작동하는지 설명해요.

③은 갑자기 문어가 바다에서 best adapted(가장 잘 적응한) 종이 되었다고 평가해요.

다른 종과의 비교나 적응 이야기는 글의 흐름과 관계없어요.

또 ③이 빠져야 ④의 this process(이 과정)가 ②의 "환경과 직접 상호 작용"을 바로 받아요.

그래서 정답은 ③이에요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 팔이 뇌 지시 없이 움직인다는 핵심 설명이에요.

② Instead(대신)로 팔의 역할을 설명해요.

④ this process(이 과정)로 앞 내용을 받아 빨판 역할을 말해요.

⑤ 빨판 덕분에 만지는 것을 맛본다는 결론이에요.

시험 포인트

문제 본문 다시 보기
Research on cephalopod neurobiology suggests that an octopus possesses roughly five hundred million neurons. What makes this system unusual is where many of those neurons are located. More than half are distributed throughout the arms rather than concentrated entirely in the central brain. This means that many actions do not require detailed instructions from the brain itself. 보기와 우리말 뜻

① All of an octopus's five hundred million neurons are concentrated in its central brain. (문어의 5억 개 뉴런은 모두 중앙 뇌에 모여 있다.) → 절반 이상이 팔에 있어서 틀려요.

② The arms of an octopus depend entirely on instructions from the brain for every act. (문어의 팔은 모든 행동을 뇌의 지시에 전적으로 의존한다.) → 많은 행동이 뇌 지시가 필요 없다고 해서 틀려요.

③ The primary and only function of an octopus's suckers is to provide a strong physical grip. (빨판의 주된 유일한 기능은 강하게 잡는 것이다.) → 화학 신호도 감지해서 틀려요.

④ Marine biologists have found that octopuses can detect chemical signals through brain activities. (해양 생물학자들은 문어가 뇌 활동으로 화학 신호를 감지한다는 것을 알아냈다.) → 뇌가 아니라 촉각(빨판)으로 감지해요.

⑤ An octopus can effectively taste what it touches because many neurons are located in its arms. (문어는 많은 뉴런이 팔에 있어서 만지는 것을 사실상 맛볼 수 있다.) → 본문 내용과 맞아요.

답의 근거 (본문)

More than half are distributed throughout the arms rather than concentrated entirely in the central brain. (뉴런의 절반 이상이 중앙 뇌에 모두 모여 있지 않고 팔 전체에 퍼져 있어요.)

These structures provide strong grip but also detect chemical signals on surfaces they contact. (이 구조(빨판)는 강하게 잡아 줄 뿐 아니라, 닿는 표면의 화학 신호도 감지해요.)

In practical terms, an octopus can effectively taste what it touches. (실제로 말하면, 문어는 자기가 만지는 것을 사실상 맛볼 수 있어요.)

쉬운 풀이

본문은 뉴런 절반 이상이 팔에 있고, 팔의 빨판이 닿는 표면의 화학 신호를 감지한다고 해요.

그래서 문어는 can effectively taste what it touches(만지는 것을 사실상 맛볼 수 있어요).

⑤는 이 두 내용을 이어서 말한 거예요.

나머지는 모두 본문과 반대예요.

그래서 정답은 ⑤예요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① More than half are distributed throughout the arms(절반 이상이 팔에 퍼져 있어요)라고 했어요.

② do not require detailed instructions from the brain(뇌의 자세한 지시가 필요 없어요)이라고 했어요.

③ but also detect chemical signals(화학 신호도 감지해요)라고 했어요.

④ through touch(촉각을 통해)라고 했지 brain activities(뇌 활동)가 아니에요.

Consistent though he may be, some of Aristotle’s ideas meet us today as strange. He had no difficulty justifying slavery, for example, and in his era, philosophers enjoyed positions of high privilege and influence. Because of their practice of contemplation for the highest human good, philosophers were considered worthy to have all the best things in life. Somehow that assertion seems oddly self-serving. It serves us to remember that every great thinker is of her own time and speaks to a particular audience, and certainly Aristotle is no exception. Human flourishing as he understood it probably would not be nearly as inclusive as we would want it to be today. It serves us to remember that every great thinker is of her own time and speaks to a particular audience, and certainly Aristotle is no exception. (모든 위대한 사상가는 자기 시대에 속하고 특정한 청중에게 말한다는 것을 기억하면 도움이 돼요. 아리스토텔레스도 분명 예외가 아니에요.)

As a result(그 결과) 뒤에는 "가치는 있지만 그 시대의 편견을 담고 있다"는 결론이 와야 해요.

그래서 정답은 ④예요.

Take, for example, someone who is going through a divorce. This is often an adverse event, and yet, it can be construed in different ways. Like all historians, social historians draw from a variety of primary sources. Social history often emphasizes ordinary people rather than elites. Yet, in most places and times, nonelites lacked access to education and, thus, did not produce substantial amounts of documentation. Even when they did produce documentation, these materials rarely ended up in archives where they could be preserved and made available to researchers. Because of such biases in the historical record, social historians have to be very creative in their work. Like other historians, social historians analyze textual sources produced by government officials, employers, and other elites. Yet rather than simply take such sources at face value, they read them “against the grain”. So, for example, a social historian will read legal documents not only for the “facts” provided by slaveowners; she will also examine the silences, interruptions, rumors, and other details that offer hints about the enslaved peoples’ attitudes and actions. 문제 본문 다시 보기
Dolphins’ brains have evolved specialized areas dedicated to echolocation, allowing them to process acoustic information more quickly and with less energy. One key adaptation is the structure of the dolphin’s auditory pathway. The signals from the ears are rapidly transmitted to the auditory cortex, the brain region responsible for processing sound. However, unlike many other mammals, dolphins have a highly specialized auditory cortex with distinct areas dedicated to processing different aspects of echolocation signals. Some areas may be responsible for analyzing the timing of echoes, while others focus on the frequency content or the intensity of the sound. This specialization allows the dolphin’s brain to process echo information in parallel, rather than sequentially. Imagine a factory assembly line, where different workers are responsible for different tasks. By dividing the work among specialized areas, the brain can process information much faster and more efficiently. This parallel processing reduces the cognitive load associated with echolocation, allowing dolphins to react quickly to changes in their environment.

보기와 우리말 뜻

① One Signal, One Brain Region (하나의 신호, 하나의 뇌 영역) → 여러 구역이 나눠 처리해요.
