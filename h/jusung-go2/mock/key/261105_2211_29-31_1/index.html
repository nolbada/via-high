문제 본문 다시 보기
Pre-emption means that a strategy is designed to prevent a rival from starting some particular activity. In some case a pre-emptive move may simply be an announcement of some intent that might discourage rivals from doing the same. The idea of pre-emption implies that timing is sometimes very important — a decision or an action at one point in time might be much more rewarding than doing it at a different time point. Pre-emption may involve up-weighting advertising for a period before and during when a new entrant launches into a market. The intent is to make it more difficult for the new entrant’s advertising to make an impression on potential buyers. Product proliferation is another potential pre-emption strategy. The general idea is to launch a large variety of product variants so that there is very little in the way of market demand that is not accommodated. Arguably, if a market is already filled with product variants it is more difficult for competitors to find untapped pockets of market demand. *pre-emption: 선매 행위 **proliferation: 확산

③ 장점과 단점을 비교하는 내용은 없어요.

④ 타이밍이 중요하다는 말은 일부일 뿐, 중요한 이유가 글 전체 내용은 아니에요.

📌 2211-29번 글 내용 정리

선매 행위(pre-emption)는 경쟁자가 어떤 활동을 시작하지 못하도록 먼저 손을 쓰는 전략이에요. 계획을 미리 발표하거나, 새 경쟁자가 들어올 때 광고를 늘리거나, 여러 종류의 제품을 쫙 깔아 두는 방법이 있어요. 시장이 내 제품으로 꽉 차 있으면 경쟁자가 비집고 들어갈 빈자리를 찾기 어려워요. 그래서 언제 행동하느냐, 즉 타이밍이 아주 중요해요.

글의 흐름

정의: 선매 행위는 경쟁자의 활동을 미리 막는 전략
▶
방법 1: 의도를 먼저 발표해서 경쟁자를 단념시키기
▶
방법 2: 새 진입자가 올 때 광고 비중 높이기
▶
방법 3: 다양한 제품을 깔아 시장 빈틈 없애기(제품 확산)
▶
결과: 경쟁자는 남은 시장 수요를 찾기 어려움

prevent A from -ing A가 ~하지 못하게 막다discourage A from -ing A가 ~하는 것을 단념시키다entrant (새로) 들어온 사람·회사, 진입자variant 변형, 다른 형태untapped 아직 손대지 않은, 개발되지 않은

보기와 우리말 뜻

경쟁자가 하는 행동을 단념시킬 수도 있다. (discourage rivals)

No learning is possible without an error signal. Organisms only learn when events violate their expectations. In other words, surprise is one of the fundamental drivers of learning. Imagine hearing a series of identical notes, AAAAA. Each note draws out a response in the auditory areas of your brain – but as the notes repeat, those responses progressively decrease. This is called “adaptation,” a deceptively simple phenomenon that shows that your brain is learning to anticipate the next event. Suddenly, the note changes: AAAAA#. Your primary auditory cortex immediately shows a strong surprise reaction: not only does the adaptation fade away, but additional neurons begin to vigorously fire in response to the unexpected sound. And it is not just repetition that leads to adaptation: what matters is whether the notes are predictable. For instance, if you hear an alternating set of notes, such as ABABA, your brain gets used to this alternation, and the activity in your auditory areas again decreases. This time, however, it is an unexpected repetition, such as ABABB, that triggers a surprise response.

문제 본문 다시 보기
No learning is possible without an error signal. Organisms only learn when events violate their expectations. In other words, surprise is one of the fundamental drivers of learning. Imagine hearing a series of identical notes, AAAAA. Each note draws out a response in the auditory areas of your brain — but as the notes repeat, those responses progressively decrease. This is called “adaptation,” a deceptively simple phenomenon that shows that your brain is learning to anticipate the next event. Suddenly, the note changes: AAAAA#. Your primary auditory cortex immediately shows a strong surprise reaction: not only does the adaptation fade away, but additional neurons begin to vigorously fire in response to the unexpected sound. And it is not just repetition that leads to adaptation: what matters is whether the notes are predictable. For instance, if you hear an alternating set of notes, such as ABABA, your brain gets used to this alternation, and the activity in your auditory areas again decreases. This time, however, it is an unexpected repetition, such as ABABB, that triggers a surprise response.

보기와 우리말 뜻

When the sound changes suddenly, the adaptation gradually disappears and additional neurons begin to be activated strenuously. (소리가 갑자기 바뀌면 적응이 사라지고 추가 신경 세포들이 힘차게 활성화되기 시작한다.)

As the same sound is repeated, the responses within the auditory areas of the brain gradually diminish. (같은 소리가 반복되면 뇌 청각 영역의 반응이 점점 줄어든다.)

Adaptation is a phenomenon that indicates that the brain is learning to predict the next event. (적응은 뇌가 다음 일을 예측하는 법을 배우고 있다는 것을 보여 주는 현상이다.)

What causes adaptation is not the predictability of the sound, but a simple repetition. (적응을 일으키는 것은 소리의 예측 가능성이 아니라 단순한 반복이다.)

An unanticipated repetition such as ABABB causes a reaction of surprise. (ABABB 같은 예상치 못한 반복은 놀람 반응을 일으킨다.)

② 음이 반복되면 반응이 점점 줄어든다고 했어요.

③ 적응은 뇌가 다음 일을 예상하는 법을 배우고 있음을 보여 준다고 했어요.
