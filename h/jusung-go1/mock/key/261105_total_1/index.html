We might not think of ourselves as global citizens yet, but it’s both necessary and possible to do so. (A) This doesn’t mean that global citizenship replaces these other identities. There’s not a single story about our identities. Instead, it’s precisely because we have obligations to our families, those in our community, and those around the world. (B) Some people are skeptical about the idea of global citizenship, but for most of human history, people didn’t think of themselves as citizens of nation-states. After all, for most of human history, nation-states didn’t exist. (C) The rise of nation-states over the last few centuries has led people to add state citizenship to other identities, such as family, people group, or religion. Given the connections between people around the world and the common challenges we face, we need to layer global citizenship on to these other identities.
정답: ③

보기와 우리말 뜻

(A) - (C) - (B) → 정체성 대체 아님 → 국민국가와 세계 시민 → 의심하는 사람들

(B) - (A) - (C) → 의심하는 사람들 → 정체성 대체 아님 → 국민국가와 세계 시민

(B) - (C) - (A) → 의심하는 사람들(국민국가도 새롭다) → 국민국가와 세계 시민 정체성 더하기 → 정체성 대체 아님

(C) - (A) - (B) → 국민국가와 세계 시민 → 정체성 대체 아님 → 의심하는 사람들

(C) - (B) - (A) → 국민국가와 세계 시민 → 의심하는 사람들 → 정체성 대체 아님

답의 근거 (본문)

Some people are skeptical about the idea of global citizenship, but for most of human history, people didn’t think of themselves as citizens of nation-states. (어떤 사람들은 세계 시민이라는 생각에 회의적이지만, 인류 역사 대부분 동안 사람들은 자신을 국민국가의 시민이라고 생각하지 않았어요.)

This doesn’t mean that global citizenship replaces these other identities. (이것은 세계 시민이 이 다른 정체성들을 대신한다는 뜻은 아니에요.)

쉬운 풀이

주어진 글은 '세계 시민이라고 생각하는 것은 필요하고 가능하다'는 주장이에요. (B)는 '의심하는 사람도 있지만, 국가 시민이라는 생각도 원래 없었다'고 답해요. (C)는 국민국가가 생기며 국가 시민 정체성이 더해졌듯이 세계 시민 정체성도 겹쳐야 한다고 말해요. (A)는 'These other identities(이 다른 정체성들)'로 (C)의 가족, 민족, 종교 등을 받아 '대신하는 것이 아니다'라고 보충해요. 그래서 (B)-(C)-(A)예요.

① (A)의 'these other identities'가 가리키는 정체성이 앞에 나오지 않아서 어색해요.

② (A)가 (C)보다 먼저 오면 'these other identities'가 무엇인지 알 수 없어요.

④ (C)가 먼저 오면 주어진 글의 주장 뒤에 (B)의 반론이 놓이지 못해 흐름이 어색해요.

⑤ (C)가 먼저 오면 주어진 글과 이어지는 (B)의 반론이 뒤로 밀려요.

다음 글의 밑줄 친 (A)If you keep playing the same game, you’ll win the same prizes.가 의미하는 바로 알맞은 것은?

문제 본문 다시 보기
Among unsatisfied professionals, there’s a repeating theme — the belief that someday (a mysterious day in the future) things will miraculously change and they’ll stumble into the perfect job or career. At the same time, they (typically) do little to meaningfully alter their current situation. They don’t like their job, so what do they do? They head to job boards and search for roles with a similar job title, hoping that the universe will sprinkle a bit of magic on their career if they just hit the refresh button in a different department or company. Efforts like this make professionals feel like they are doing something, when really they’re just setting themselves up to take a new role and quickly become just as disenchanted as they were before. It’s like rearranging deck chairs on the Titanic. You’ve got to completely switch up your approach. (A)<u>If you keep playing the same game, you’ll win the same prizes</u>. *stumble into: ~을 우연히 마주치다 **disenchanted: 환상이 깨진
정답: ⑤

보기와 우리말 뜻

Finding a new position will eventually resolve career dissatisfaction. → 새 일자리를 찾으면 결국 직장 불만이 해결될 것이다

Maintaining a familiar career path will ensure long-term job security. → 익숙한 진로를 유지하면 오랫동안 안정된 직장이 보장된다

Professional satisfaction depends more on luck than personal decisions. → 직업 만족은 개인의 결정보다 운에 더 달려 있다

Changing employers regularly will provide more opportunities for success. → 회사를 자주 바꾸면 성공 기회가 더 늘어난다

Unless you change your approach to your career, you will face similar dissatisfaction. → 커리어에 대한 접근 방식을 바꾸지 않으면 비슷한 불만을 겪게 될 것이다

답의 근거 (본문)

Efforts like this make professionals feel like they are doing something, when really they’re just setting themselves up to take a new role and quickly become just as disenchanted as they were before. (이런 노력은 직장인들이 뭔가 하고 있다는 기분이 들게 하지만, 사실은 새 일자리를 얻고 곧 전과 똑같이 환상이 깨질 준비를 하는 것일 뿐이에요.)

You’ve got to completely switch up your approach. (접근 방식을 완전히 바꿔야 해요.)

If you keep playing the same game, you’ll win the same prizes. (같은 게임을 계속하면 같은 상을 받게 될 거예요.)

쉬운 풀이

'같은 게임을 계속하면 같은 상을 받는다'는 말은 '같은 방식으로 계속하면 같은 결과가 나온다'는 비유예요. 바로 앞에서 '비슷한 일자리로 옮겨도 곧 또 실망하게 되니 접근 방식을 완전히 바꿔야 한다'고 했어요. 그러니 '접근 방식을 바꾸지 않으면 비슷한 불만을 겪는다'가 맞아요.

다른 보기는 왜 아닐까요?

① 새 일자리로 옮기면 해결된다는 내용은 글이 말한 것과 정반대예요.

② 안정된 직장 이야기는 글에 없고, 익숙한 방식을 유지하면 계속 불만이라는 뜻이에요.

③ 운 이야기는 글에 없어요. 글은 접근 방식(개인의 선택)을 바꾸라고 해요.

④ 자주 옮기는 것은 글이 '겉만 바꾸는 노력'이라고 한 내용이에요.

문제 본문 다시 보기
Sociologists argue that our perception and usage of time are not just basic properties of nature, like a clock ticking away in the background, nor are they simply psychological phenomena of individual experience. Instead, how we perceive and understand time is to a large degree shaped by tradition and cultural norms. Eviatar Zerubavel’s work, The Seven Day Circle: The History and Meaning of the Week, demonstrates how the week, a fundamental unit of time, is a social invention that varies across cultures, influencing how people structure their activities and rhythms of life. Why do we consider a nine-to-five job routine? Why don’t many of us work on the weekends? Why do we usually pay our bills at the end of the month? Why is the retirement age sixty-five? These numbers and patterns aren’t really dictated by nature or any essential human characteristic, but are rather social creations that we internalize and come to accept as “normal.”

문제 본문 다시 보기
In recent years, the number of illegal actions in particular at the corporate level has grown, or at least the number of cases that came to light increased. Such misconduct has often only been exposed because brave informants have risked a great deal in order to put an end to it. Hence, it is no surprise that whistleblower protection has come into focus. If violations of law and order go undetected and unpunished for years because employees of a company fear to report illegal activities for fear of reprisals or consequences, it can have several negative effects. It can harm the company itself, but also third-parties, the economy in general, the general public, and even the rule of law could be affected. Protecting those who would assist in reducing unlawful or even criminal behaviour has two main functions: Protecting the individual’s actions as well as protecting the public good. A well-balanced whistleblower protection may have positive effects on both of these. [from Whistleblower Protection and the Right of Access by Stephan Koloßa]

문제 본문 다시 보기
Patterns such as infinite scroll, autoplaying videos, or requiring an account just to make a purchase all interfere with the user’s control over their own experience. From a sustainability perspective, many of these patterns encourage overconsumption of data and attention. Infinite scroll keeps loading new content whether the user wants it or not, increasing data usage and device processing. Autoplay forces media to load and play even for people who may never watch it. These choices consume more energy on both the user’s device and the servers delivering the content. Take the decision by a popular music streaming platform to replace static album artwork with short video loops by default. This change significantly increases data usage and battery consumption, while the option to disable it is buried deep in the settings. As a result, most users consume more resources unless they actively search for a way to opt out. It is important to acknowledge that web design always nudges behavior in some way. Showing consequences and highlighting options are all forms of influence. The ethical line is not whether nudging exists, but whether it is transparent, reversible, and aligned with the user’s interests. In many cases, the most responsible approach is to make the lower-impact option the default, while keeping alternatives clearly available. Defaults are powerful, and when chosen carefully, they can reduce environmental impact without removing user agency. [from Building the Sustainable Web by Chris Chinchilla]
