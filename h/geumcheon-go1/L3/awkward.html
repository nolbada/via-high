(1) mental → physical(정신적인 → 신체적인)(2) conventional → innovative(전통적인 → 혁신적인)(3) limited → limitless(제한된 → 무한한)

어법 1번 글
(1) though → despite((~이지만) 어려움이 그들의 삶에 → 그들 삶의 어려움에도 불구하고)(2) have → has(이야기가 만들어 왔다 (복수 동사) → 이야기가 만들어 줬다 (단수 동사))
어법 2번 글
(1) which → where(앨라배마, 그것이 그는 일자리를 얻었다 → 앨라배마, 거기서 그는 일자리를 얻었다)(2) when → that(너무 아파서 일할 수 없을 때 그는 돌아섰다 → 너무 아파서 일할 수 없게 된 후에야 그는 돌아섰다)(3) → been(그들은 농장에서 자주 사용했다 → 그들은 농장에서 자주 사용되어 왔다)
어법 3번 글
(1) which → where(노바스코샤, 그것이 그녀는 미래의 남편을 만났다 → 노바스코샤, 거기서 그녀는 미래의 남편을 만났다)(2) generate → generating(단순한 형태, 만들어라 독창적인 → 단순한 형태, 독창적인 ...을 만들어 내며)(3) Despite → Although((~에도 불구하고) 그녀의 신체적 제약이 가뒀다 → 비록 그녀의 신체적 제약이 가뒀지만)
어법 4번 글
(1) that → which(파리, 그것은 드물었다 (that) → 파리, 그것은 드문 일이었다 (which))(2) focusing → focus(오로지 집중하는 중이었다 → 오로지 집중해야 했다)(3) seeing → seen(그녀의 작품에서 보는 것처럼 (-ing) → 그녀의 작품에서 보이듯이)

문맥 1번 글어색한 곳 2개

Welcome to the Dream Art Gallery! I’m Isabel Williams, the docent for the From Shadows to Spotlights exhibit. Today, you’re going to meet three amazing artists who never gave up on their art, despite successes1 in their lives. Each artist has a unique painting style and story that has made their work slightly2 valued. Let’s explore each artist’s life and artwork.

글의 흐름 전시를 소개하는 도슨트(전시 해설가)의 인사말이에요. 세 예술가가 어려움 속에서도 포기하지 않았고, 그래서 작품이 높이 평가받는다는 흐름이에요. despite (~에도 불구하고) 뒤에는 '이겨 낸 어려움'이, '높이 평가받는'이라는 결과에는 정도가 센 말이 와야 해요.
그대로 두는 부분 amazing (놀라운)은 예술가를 소개하는 칭찬이라 자연스러워요. unique (독특한)는 '각자 자기만의 화풍'이라는 뜻으로 뒤의 style and story (화풍과 이야기)와 잘 맞아요.
(1)어색한 말successes (성공) → challenges (어려움(도전))
고친 문장Today, you're going to meet three amazing artists who never gave up on their art, despite challenges in their lives.
오늘은 인생의 역경에도 불구하고 결코 예술을 포기하지 않았던 세 명의 놀라운 예술가들을 만나 보려고 한다.
despite (~에도 불구하고)는 '~인데도'라는 뜻이라서 뒤에 '힘든 일'이 와야 해요. 바로 앞에 never gave up on their art (예술을 결코 포기하지 않았다)가 있으니, 포기하고 싶을 만큼 힘든 challenges (어려움)가 와야 자연스러워요. successes (성공)는 포기할 이유가 되지 않아서 흐름이 깨져요.
↑ 빠른정답
(2)어색한 말slightly (약간) → highly (매우 높이)
고친 문장Each artist has a unique painting style and story that has made their work highly valued.
각 예술가에게는 독특한 화풍과 그들의 작품을 높이 평가받게 만들어 준 이야기가 있다.
문장 끝이 '그 이야기가 작품을 ~ 평가받게 만들었다'예요. 이 글은 예술가들을 크게 소개하는 글이라 highly valued (높이 평가받는)가 맞아요. slightly (약간)는 '조금만 평가받는'이 되어서 칭찬하는 분위기와 맞지 않아요.
↑ 빠른정답
문맥 2번 글어색한 곳 3개

Bill Traylor The first artist we’re going to examine is Bill Traylor. Traylor was born into slavery in the U.S. in 1853 and spent his early life working on a cotton farm in Alabama. Although he became a free man after the American Civil War, he still had to face racial discrimination, working for very high1 wages on the farm. Later, when he was around 70 years old, Traylor moved to the city of Montgomery, Alabama, where he found a job in a factory. It wasn’t until he was 85 years old and became too well2 to work that he turned to drawing to express his life experiences. Now, we’re going to look at some of his paintings, starting with Mean Dog and Man and Large Dog. Traylor had a strong fear of dogs because they had often been used on farms to watch and hunt slaves. His fear of dogs is expressed strongly in these pieces. In contrast, Woman with Purse and Man with Umbrella portrays the free lives of African Americans that he observed on the streets of Montgomery. Using simple shapes and colors, Traylor captured simple3 moments in American history from slavery to freedom. As a result, he’s now considered an important figure in American folk art.

글의 흐름 Bill Traylor (빌 트레일러)가 노예로 태어나 어렵게 살다가 늦게 그림을 시작한 이야기예요. 힘든 삶을 말하는 부분인지, 나중에 인정받는 부분인지 나눠서 읽으면 어색한 말이 보여요.
그대로 두는 부분 examine (살펴보다)은 '이제 이 예술가를 살펴보겠다'는 안내라 맞아요. face (직면하다)는 racial discrimination (인종차별)과 어울려요. strong (강한)은 fear (두려움)를 꾸미고, important (중요한)는 마지막 평가 문장에 알맞아요.
(1)어색한 말high (높은) → low (낮은)
고친 문장Although he became a free man after the American Civil War, he still had to face racial discrimination, working for very low wages on the farm.
그는 미국 남북전쟁 이후 자유의 몸이 되었지만, 농장에서 매우 낮은 임금을 받고 일하며 여전히 인종차별에 직면해야 했다.
앞에서 노예로 태어나 고생했고, 자유의 몸이 된 뒤에도 still had to face racial discrimination (여전히 인종차별에 직면해야 했다)고 해요. 이 힘든 흐름에서 농장 임금도 very low wages (아주 낮은 임금)여야 맞아요. high (높은)는 좋은 대우라서 앞뒤와 안 맞아요.
↑ 빠른정답
(2)어색한 말well (건강한) → ill (아픈)
고친 문장It wasn't until he was 85 years old and became too ill to work that he turned to drawing to express his life experiences.
85세가 되고 몸이 너무 아파서 일을 할 수 없게 된 후에야 그는 자신의 인생 경험을 표현하기 위해 그림으로 눈을 돌렸다.
'85세가 되고 너무 ~해서 일을 할 수 없게 된 후에야 그림으로 눈을 돌렸다'는 문장이에요. too ~ to work (너무 ~해서 일할 수 없는)에는 일을 못 할 이유가 와야 하니 ill (아픈)이 맞아요. well (건강한)이면 일을 할 수 있다는 뜻이 되어 too ~ to와 충돌해요.
↑ 빠른정답
(3)어색한 말simple (단순한) → complex (복잡한)
고친 문장Using simple shapes and colors, Traylor captured complex moments in American history from slavery to freedom.
Traylor는 단순한 형태와 색채를 사용하여 노예 제도에서 자유에 이르는 미국 역사의 복잡한 순간을 포착했다.
앞에 Using simple shapes and colors (단순한 형태와 색채를 사용하여)가 있어요. 그런데 포착한 것이 slavery to freedom (노예 제도에서 자유까지)라는 큰 역사의 순간이니, 단순한 도구로 복잡한 순간을 담았다는 대비가 자연스러워요. simple moments (단순한 순간)이면 '단순한 걸로 단순한 걸 그렸다'가 되어 의미가 약해져요.
↑ 빠른정답
문맥 3번 글어색한 곳 3개

Maud Lewis Next, we have Maud Lewis, a renowned artist known for her heart-warming paintings. Born in a small Canadian town in 1903, Lewis suffered from mental1 weaknesses such as distorted shoulders and fingers. This limited her mobility and caused her to drop out of school. To make a living, she began to paint and sell Christmas cards. When her parents passed away, Lewis went to live with her aunt in Digby, Nova Scotia, where she met her future husband, Everett Lewis. After marrying, the couple spent the rest of their lives there, and Lewis continued to paint. She often depicted the Digby landscapes in paintings such as Edge of Digby Harbor. Her artwork used a mixture of bright and vivid oil paints and simple forms, generating an original, conventional2 style. Although her physical limitations confined her to a small cottage, her talent and imagination were both limited3. In Red Sleigh, red maple leaves appear on a special winter landscape, and Pair of Oxen shows decorated cows standing in a flower field. With these features, Lewis’ paintings create a magical quality, like that of a fairy tale. As her paintings gradually gained popularity, her story inspired many people and was later made into books and movies. Maud Lewis expressed her love for the world through her paintings and became an iconic figure in Canadian folk art.

글의 흐름 Maud Lewis (모드 루이스)는 몸이 불편했지만 밝고 독창적인 그림으로 사랑받은 화가예요. '몸의 한계'와 '마음과 재능의 한계 없음'이 대비되는 글이라, 한계를 말하는 곳과 자유를 말하는 곳을 구분하면 돼요.
그대로 두는 부분 renowned (유명한)는 뒤의 known for (~로 알려진)와 같은 뜻이라 맞아요. 앞쪽(첫 번째)의 limited (제한했다)는 'This limited her mobility (이것이 이동성을 제한했다)'라서 정상이에요. iconic (상징적인)은 마지막의 '캐나다 민속 예술의 대표 인물'과 잘 맞아요.
(1)어색한 말mental (정신적인) → physical (신체적인)
고친 문장Born in a small Canadian town in 1903, Lewis suffered from physical weaknesses such as distorted shoulders and fingers.
1903년 캐나다의 작은 마을에서 태어난 Lewis는 기형적인 어깨와 손가락과 같은 신체적 약점으로 고통받았다.
뒤에 such as distorted shoulders and fingers (기형적인 어깨와 손가락 같은)가 나와요. 어깨와 손가락은 몸이니까 physical (신체적인)이 맞아요. 또 다음 문장 This limited her mobility (이것이 이동성을 제한했다)도 몸의 이야기이고, 아래의 physical limitations (신체적 제약)와도 이어져요.
↑ 빠른정답
(2)어색한 말conventional (전통적인) → innovative (혁신적인)
고친 문장Her artwork used a mixture of bright and vivid oil paints and simple forms, generating an original, innovative style.
그녀의 작품은 밝고 선명한 유화물감의 혼합과 단순한 형태를 사용하여 독창적이고 혁신적인 스타일을 만들어 냈다.
밝고 선명한 유화물감과 단순한 형태로 만든 스타일이니 original (독창적인)과 짝을 이루는 innovative (혁신적인)가 맞아요. conventional (전통적인)은 original과 뜻이 반대라서 an original, conventional style (독창적이고 전통적인 스타일)이 되어 어색해요.
↑ 빠른정답
(3)어색한 말limited (제한된) → limitless (무한한)
고친 문장Although her physical limitations confined her to a small cottage, her talent and imagination were both limitless.
비록 신체적 제약들은 그녀를 작은 오두막집에 가두었지만 그녀의 재능과 상상력은 둘 다 무한했다.
Although (비록 ~이지만)로 시작하니 뒤는 앞과 반대가 되어야 해요. 몸은 작은 오두막에 갇혀 있었지만(physical limitations (신체적 제약)) 재능과 상상력은 limitless (한계가 없는)였다는 대비가 되어야 해요. limited (제한된)를 쓰면 앞뒤가 같은 방향이 되어 Although가 어색해져요.

문맥 4번 글어색한 곳 3개

Anna Ancher Now let’s take a look at the final artist of this exhibition: Anna Ancher, a famous painter from Denmark. When observing her paintings, you may notice a common theme—they all feature female figures. Born in Skagen, Denmark, in 1859, she later moved to Copenhagen to attend a private painting school. After that, she even studied abroad in Paris, which was unusual for women at the time. Thanks to her mother’s discouragement1, she was able to take advantage of these opportunities. Even after getting married, Ancher persisted in painting, objecting to the social pressure that married women were to solely focus on household duties. Ancher differed from other artists of that era, who depicted women as still life subjects. In contrast, she showcased them as passive2 participants in everyday tasks, as seen in her works The Maid in the Kitchen and Sewing Fisherman’s Wife. She also skillfully explored light and color, contributing to the rich Impressionist movement in Denmark. In her painting Sunlight in the Blue Room, the reflection of the sunlight on the blue wall is stunningly portrayed. Ancher accepted3 the conventional roles of women in the 20th century and displayed her exceptional artistic talent. Her paintings continue to amaze us to this day.

Thank you for joining this guided tour, and I hope my explanations have aided you in appreciating these paintings. Please take some time to further explore the exhibition.

글의 흐름 Anna Ancher (안나 앙케르)는 여성이 공부하기 어려운 시대에 유학까지 한 화가예요. 이 글은 '시대의 편견에 맞선 사람'이라는 흐름이라, 편견을 따르는 말이 나오면 어색해요.
그대로 두는 부분 objecting (반대하다)은 '사회적 압력에 맞서'라서 맞아요. differed (달랐다)는 뒤의 In contrast (이와 대조적으로)와 이어지고, exceptional (뛰어난)은 재능을 칭찬하는 말이라 자연스러워요.
(1)어색한 말discouragement (낙담시킴(말림)) → encouragement (격려)
고친 문장Thanks to her mother's encouragement, she was able to take advantage of these opportunities.
어머니의 격려 덕분에 그녀는 이러한 기회를 활용할 수 있었다.
뒤에 she was able to take advantage of these opportunities (이 기회들을 활용할 수 있었다)가 있어요. Thanks to (~덕분에)는 좋은 이유 앞에 쓰니 encouragement (격려)가 맞아요. discouragement (낙담시킴)이면 말리는 건데, 그 덕에 유학을 갔다는 말은 안 맞아요.
↑ 빠른정답
(2)어색한 말passive (수동적인) → active (적극적인)
고친 문장In contrast, she showcased them as active participants in everyday tasks, as seen in her works The Maid in the Kitchen and Sewing Fisherman's Wife.
이와는 대조적으로, 〈부엌의 하녀〉와 〈바느질하는 어부의 아내〉라는 작품에서 볼 수 있듯 그녀는 일상 작업의 적극적인 참여자로서의 여성을 보여 주었다.
앞 문장에서 다른 화가들은 여성을 still life subjects (정물화 소재)처럼 가만히 있는 대상으로 그렸다고 해요. 이어서 In contrast (이와 대조적으로)가 나오니 Ancher는 active (적극적인) 참여자로 보여 줘야 해요. passive (수동적인)이면 다른 화가들과 똑같아져서 대조가 사라져요.
↑ 빠른정답
(3)어색한 말accepted (받아들였다) → challenged (이의를 제기했다(맞섰다))
고친 문장Ancher challenged the conventional roles of women in the 20th century and displayed her exceptional artistic talent.
Ancher는 20세기 여성의 전통적인 역할에 이의를 제기하며 뛰어난 예술적 재능을 보여 주었다.
이 글은 편견에 맞선 Ancher의 이야기예요. 앞에서도 objecting to the social pressure (사회적 압력에 저항하며), In contrast (대조적으로)로 이어졌어요. 그러니 the conventional roles (전통적인 역할)를 challenged (이의를 제기했다)해야 맞아요. accepted (받아들였다)이면 지금까지의 흐름과 정반대예요.

어법상 어색한 것 찾기 · 해설

어법 1번 글어색한 곳 2개

Welcome to the Dream Art Gallery! I’m Isabel Williams, the docent for the From Shadows to Spotlights exhibit. Today, you’re going to meet three amazing artists who never gave up on their art, though challenges in their lives1. Each artist has a unique painting style and story that have made their work highly valued.2 Let’s explore each artist’s life and artwork.

글의 흐름 도슨트의 인사말이라 짧은 문장이 이어져요. 밑줄 친 네 문장 중 '~에도 불구하고'를 이어 주는 말과, 동사가 주어와 맞는지를 살펴보세요.

(1)고치기though ((~이지만) 어려움이 그들의 삶에) → despite (그들 삶의 어려움에도 불구하고)

Bill Traylor The first artist we’re going to examine is Bill Traylor. Traylor was born into slavery in the U.S. in 1853 and spent his early life working on a cotton farm in Alabama. Although he became a free man after the American Civil War, he still had to face racial discrimination, working for very low wages on the farm. Later, when he was around 70 years old, Traylor moved to the city of Montgomery, Alabama, which he found a job in a factory.1 It wasn’t until he was 85 years old and became too ill to work when he turned to drawing to express his life experiences.2 Now, we’re going to look at some of his paintings, starting with Mean Dog and Man and Large Dog. Traylor had a strong fear of dogs because they had often used on farms to watch and hunt slaves.3 His fear of dogs is expressed strongly in these pieces. In contrast, Woman with Purse and Man with Umbrella portrays the free lives of African Americans that he observed on the streets of Montgomery. Using simple shapes and colors, Traylor captured complex moments in American history from slavery to freedom. As a result, he’s now considered an important figure in American folk art.

글의 흐름 Bill Traylor의 삶을 시간 순서로 소개하는 글이에요. 장소 뒤에서 '거기서'로 이어 주는 말, 'It wasn't until ~ (~하고 나서야)'의 짝, 동사의 모양(수동)을 차례로 살펴보세요.

Maud Lewis Next, we have Maud Lewis, a renowned artist known for her heart-warming paintings. Born in a small Canadian town in 1903, Lewis suffered from physical weaknesses such as distorted shoulders and fingers. This limited her mobility and caused her to drop out of school. To make a living, she began to paint and sell Christmas cards. When her parents passed away, Lewis went to live with her aunt in Digby, Nova Scotia, which she met her future husband, Everett Lewis1. After marrying, the couple spent the rest of their lives there, and Lewis continued to paint. She often depicted the Digby landscapes in paintings such as Edge of Digby Harbor. Her artwork used a mixture of bright and vivid oil paints and simple forms, generate an original, innovative style.2 Despite her physical limitations confined her to a small cottage, her talent and imagination were both limitless.3 In Red Sleigh, red maple leaves appear on a special winter landscape, and Pair of Oxen shows decorated cows standing in a flower field. With these features, Lewis’ paintings create a magical quality, like that of a fairy tale. As her paintings gradually gained popularity, her story inspired many people and was later made into books and movies. Maud Lewis expressed her love for the world through her paintings and became an iconic figure in Canadian folk art.

Anna Ancher Now let’s take a look at the final artist of this exhibition: Anna Ancher, a famous painter from Denmark. When observing her paintings, you may notice a common theme—they all feature female figures. Born in Skagen, Denmark, in 1859, she later moved to Copenhagen to attend a private painting school. After that, she even studied abroad in Paris, that was unusual for women at the time.1 Thanks to her mother’s encouragement, she was able to take advantage of these opportunities. Even after getting married, Ancher persisted in painting, objecting to the social pressure that married women were to solely focusing on household duties2. Ancher differed from other artists of that era, who depicted women as still life subjects. In contrast, she showcased them as active participants in everyday tasks, as seeing in her works The Maid in the Kitchen and Sewing Fisherman’s Wife3. She also skillfully explored light and color, contributing to the rich Impressionist movement in Denmark. In her painting Sunlight in the Blue Room, the reflection of the sunlight on the blue wall is stunningly portrayed. Ancher challenged the conventional roles of women in the 20th century and displayed her exceptional artistic talent. Her paintings continue to amaze us to this day.

Thank you for joining this guided tour, and I hope my explanations have aided you in appreciating these paintings. Please take some time to further explore the exhibition.

(3)고치기seeing (그녀의 작품에서 보는 것처럼 (-ing)) → seen (그녀의 작품에서 보이듯이)
틀린 문장she showcased them as active participants in everyday tasks, as seeing in her works The Maid in the Kitchen and Sewing Fisherman’s Wife.고친 문장she showcased them as active participants in everyday tasks, as seen in her works The Maid in the Kitchen and Sewing Fisherman’s Wife.
이와는 대조적으로, 〈부엌의 하녀〉와 〈바느질하는 어부의 아내〉라는 작품에서 볼 수 있듯 그녀는 일상 작업의 적극적인 참여자로서의 여성을 보여 주었다.
as seen in ~은 '~에서 볼 수 있듯이'라는 굳어진 표현이에요. 작품이 '보여지는' 쪽이라서 '보여진'이라는 뜻의 seen (보인)을 써요. seeing (보는)은 작품이 본다는 뜻이 되어 틀려요. 그 뒤의 her works (그녀의 작품들)는 The Maid in the Kitchen (부엌의 하녀) 같은 작품들을 가리켜요.
쉬운 문법
as seen in ~ = ~에서 볼 수 있듯이
  • as 뒤에 seen을 써서 '보이듯이'가 돼요.
  • seeing은 '보는'이라 뜻이 달라요.
As seen in the photo, he is very tall.사진에서 보이듯이 그는 매우 키가 커요.
